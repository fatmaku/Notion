## 2. Thesis: one voice, three products

All three projects start from the same place: **spoken language in family life, education and the creator economy – in German, Turkish and English.**

1. **Voice technology.** Aybal turns a family’s own books and documents into read-aloud audio with word-by-word highlighting (Aybal deck, slide 4). LiveFX turns speech into live effects in under a second (BUSINESSPLAN.md, section 1). Both rely on speech recognition, speech output and word timing.
2. **Three languages as the starting point, not as localisation.** Aybal offers 3 interface and 13 voice languages, with measured intelligibility of 98% (German) and 97% (Turkish) (Aybal deck, slides 4 and 7). LiveFX recognises German, Turkish and English automatically; its largest trigger pack is Turkish (85 of 238 triggers) (BUSINESSPLAN.md, section 3.2).
3. **One audience, three entry points.** Families read (Aybal), creators and teachers stream and teach (LiveFX), children and teenagers learn with stories in which they recognise themselves (book series).

The German-Turkish community is the launch market for both software products: Aybal launches in Germany and addresses Türkiye as a target market (Aybal deck, slides 3 and 6); LiveFX starts with Turkish-speaking creators in Türkiye and Europe (BUSINESSPLAN.md, sections 5 and 10).

## 3. The three projects

### 3.1 Aybal – “The Voice of Family Reading”

**Product.** Families upload PDFs, EPUBs, Word files, text or photos of pages; Aybal reads them aloud with word-by-word highlighting. Differentiation per the deck: natural multilingual voices, consented personal voices, user-owned content, word-level highlighting, family accounts and controls (Aybal deck, slides 4 and 7).

**Status.** Working platform with 1,779 automated tests passed; closed beta with 50 founding families from 12 October 2026; Germany launch on 20 November 2026, when revenue starts (Aybal deck, slides 2, 4, 6, 8).

**Market (Aybal deck, slides 3 and 5).**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Families with children in Germany, Türkiye, the UK, the rest of the EU and the US | 101M |
| TAM – gross family reading spend in target markets | €7.6B |
| SAM – 15% assumed willingness to pay | €1.14B |
| SOM – 4% penetration of the willing segment; directional, not a forecast | €38M |

**Pricing and margins (Aybal deck, slide 6).** Plus €9.99/month (€6.41 margin, 76%), Plus €79.99/year (75%), Family €24.99/month (€16.22 margin, 77%), Family €199.99/year. Margins are for Germany/EU web purchases.

**Scenario (Aybal deck, slides 9 and 10).** Venture case from 2,571 subscriber households in 2027 to 313,116 by 2033, with 2033 revenue of €39.37M. Break-even: strong case August 2030, venture case October 2031. Explicitly a scenario, not a forecast.

**Own round (Aybal deck, slides 11 and 12).** €250,000 pre-seed (≈ $285,000), valuation cap €1.30M pre-money (~16% equity at cap), planned close March 2027. Use of funds: team 50% (€125,000), marketing 25% (€62,500), EU voice operation and store app development 15% (€37,500), legal/trust 10% (€25,000).

**Milestone gates (Aybal deck, slide 13).** Apr 2027 activation (300 trials, ≥43% trial-to-pay, ≤25% month-one churn) · Q1 2028 retention (12-month retention ≥38%, K-factor ≥0.25, LTV/CAC ≥3) · 2029 efficiency (conversion ≥5.5%, DACH signup ≤€3.50, paid CAC ≤€54) · 2030 expansion (five markets live, platform pilot).

### 3.2 LiveFX – “Live streams that listen”

**Status.** Version 2.0, working; 238 triggers in five packs (Turkish 85, German 49, English 50, Family & Kids 27, Gaming 27), 13 scenes, 12 ambience loops, 38 sounds; used in the founder’s own streams (BUSINESSPLAN.md, sections 1 and 3.2); 240+ automated tests (PITCH-DECK.md, slide 9). **There is no revenue yet** (BUSINESSPLAN.md, section 11). ## 4. Synergies

| Synergy | How it works | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared speech and voice stack** | Speech recognition, speech output and word timing serve both products: read-aloud with word highlighting (Aybal); recognition, scenes and pronunciation cards (LiveFX WortBild) | Aybal deck slide 4; BUSINESSPLAN.md sections 3.2, 15.3 |

These synergies are **hypotheses**; they have not yet been integrated technically or measured. A shared stack requires the companies to license code and content to each other (see section 6).

## 6. Structure options

Presented neutrally. **Not a recommendation – legal and tax advice required.**

| Option | Description | For investors | Points to consider |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · One holding** | A parent company holds Aybal, LiveFX and the rights to the book series (as subsidiaries or business units) | One entry point, one cap table, synergies steered centrally, IP pooled | New valuation of the whole package; the Aybal terms (€1.30M cap) must be carried over or renegotiated; project risks are blended |
| **B · Separate companies (SPVs)** | One company per project, each with its own round | Valuation and risk chosen per project; Aybal round unchanged | More contracts; licence agreements needed for shared code and content; possible conflicts of interest between companies |
| **C · Hybrid** | Aybal round as planned in the Aybal company; LiveFX and content in a second company; licence agreement between the two | Middle path: Aybal terms stay, the rest is pooled | Transfer pricing and licence terms to be settled |

Under every option: document the chain of rights for the books, trademarks (the business plan notes “LiveFX” still needs clearance, section 4) and code ownership before closing.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation per the documents |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand and retention unproven** | Aybal earns revenue only from Nov 2026; LiveFX has no revenue yet | Aybal: beta cohorts, spend gated on conversion and churn (deck slide 14); LiveFX: free tier, packs, B2B as a second pillar (BP section 13) |
| **Key people** | Both software projects depend heavily on their founders | Aybal: priority hiring, vesting, key-person insurance planned (deck slides 14, 16); LiveFX: documentation, tests, early hiring, advisory board (BP section 13) |
| **Focus** | Three projects with a small team | Separate budgets and milestones per project; content budget kept small |
| **Voice, copyright, data protection** | Personal voices, third-party content, children as an audience | Aybal: consent controls, rights workflow, DPIA (deck slide 14); LiveFX: local processing, no accounts required (BP section 13) |
| **Platform dependency** | App stores (Aybal); TikTok/Meta/YouTube and browser speech recognition (LiveFX) | Web-first (Aybal); offline Whisper, several routes per platform (LiveFX) |
| **Türkiye FX** | Prices and revenue in TRY | Localised pricing, regular FX sensitivity review (Aybal deck slide 14) |
| **Structure and conflicts of interest** | Shared IP spread across several companies | Clear licence agreements, legal advice (section 6) |
