Your Turn

Have you ever seen a line running from your own pain to others — a moment when you recognized what you've lived through in someone else's eyes? Is there a story around you told in "the wrong words"; perhaps your own story? Do a small exercise this week: write down, one below the other, three "wrong words" that were once pinned on you ("lazy," "too sensitive," "scattered," for instance). Then beside each, place a word that sees the same trait but opens no wound ("self-protecting" instead of "lazy," "deep" instead of "too sensitive," "multi-channel" instead of "scattered"). Finally, write yourself a single sentence: for whom can you be a bridge today — and what do you promise yourself about not stepping onto that bridge before first looking after yourself?
