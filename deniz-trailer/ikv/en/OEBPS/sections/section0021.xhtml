Your Turn

Does closeness come to you, too, in two languages — both attractive and dangerous? Has there been a moment in your life when you preferred "familiar pain to unknown peace"; what does that moment tell you about what you took to be "home"? A small exercise for this week: first, think of a relationship in which you always gave in love and received nothing back, and write it in a single sentence — what was your unmet need there? Then choose, sometime this week, a single moment in which you can express a tiny need of yours ("I want to be alone right now," "would you hold me," "I don't want to do this") out loud, clearly, and plan it here. What is the smallest step you could take today toward adding yourself to the list of the ones you love?
