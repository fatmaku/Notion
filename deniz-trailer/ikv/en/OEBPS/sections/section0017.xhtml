Your Turn

Has your body, too, ever learned to read a sound, a key, a glance, the noise of a footstep as "danger"? Have you ever both loved a person and feared them at the same time — and been unable to tell anyone? Try this: Close your eyes and return to that old "click" moment, to that trembling child. As the you of today, say a single sentence to him — as if to say, "I'm with you now." Then write that sentence below. And choose one step for this week: To whom, and how, can you show safe closeness? Maybe saying to someone you trust, "I feel safe with you." Let it be small, but let it be real. Write.
