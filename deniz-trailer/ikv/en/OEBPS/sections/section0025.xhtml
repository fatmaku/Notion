Your Turn

Have you ever tried to give someone you love something you didn't have within yourself? Is there an area where you want to break a cycle — with your own child, your sibling, your own inner child? Now a small exercise: draw a line down the middle of a sheet of paper. On the left, write something that was missing in your own childhood and that you still long for (a sentence, a touch, an "I love you"). On the right, write how you might give that missing thing today to someone — or to yourself. Then choose one concrete step for this week: a message, an embrace, a true sentence spoken instead of acted. Write it below.
