Your Turn

Do you, too, carry inside you the feeling that "there is more, something beyond the words"? Is there something you have searched for outside for years — in a person, a place, a teacher, or a "great experience" — but which has perhaps always been inside you? Now do a small exercise: draw two columns on a sheet of paper. In the left column, write the people/things in your life you have gone after, imitated, traced (even a dog, by following the one it loves, arrives somewhere — but that place is not its own path). In the right column, answer a single question: "If I followed no one, what one small step would I take today?" Write that step here — and this week, take only that step, with your own foot.
