Your Turn

In your childhood, did you ever feel invisible while people were all around you? Which moment, which room, which smell comes to mind? Back then, where, into which story or hero, did you flee — and what did that escape teach you? Now a small exercise: close your eyes and, for a moment, sit that child down across from you. Ask the child the one question no one asked that day — "What really happened inside you today?" — and write the answer below, in that child's own voice. Then this week, consciously say "I see you" to one person, and once to yourself as well; note down what you feel.
