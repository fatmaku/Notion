Your Turn

Do you blame yourself for a decision you made under conditions in which you would never make the same choice today? How do you draw the line between "guilt" and "responsibility" in your own life? Now a small exercise: draw a vertical line down the middle of a sheet of paper. On the left, write: "What did the me of that day know, what did they feel, what fear were they inside of?" On the right, write: "What would the me of today say if I had known this?" Then write a single sentence to your self of that day, just as you would speak to a frightened child — without judgment.
