Your Turn

This chapter doesn't describe an "arrival" but a "ground" — and that ground can be built for you too. First, take an honest measurement: lately, how long did one of your bouts of anger last — minutes, hours, or days? Then look back: which mask did you carry longest — the worker mask, the agreeable mask, the strong mask, or something else? What did that mask protect you from, and what did it cost you? Now a mini-exercise: this week, place a "no" somewhere tiny — without explanation, without apology, as a complete sentence. In just one place. And write down the smallest step you can take today on the road from "I am wrong" to "This is who I am" — so small that you'll have no excuse not to do it. Write below; paper doesn't judge.
