Your Turn

Have you ever felt like the "wrong piece"? Do you still hear that voice — and be honest, is that voice really yours, or the echo of a sentence someone once said to you? This week, try a small experiment: when that voice rises inside you, stop and ask it, "Who are you? Who first said this sentence to me?" Then write below where you could set a small boundary this week — a "no," a "this isn't good for me," a "I don't want to be spoken to this way." Just one concrete place, just one concrete sentence. No fist needed; a word is enough.
