Your Turn

For you, does "putting down roots" mean stopping, or moving? The "keep going" voice inside you — is it calling you forward, or making you flee from something — can you honestly tell the difference? This week, try a small experiment: on one of your days, share a single thing with someone else — a wound, something you've learned, an "I'm like this too" — and then note below, in two sentences, what changed in your body (shoulder, chest, breath). And ask yourself: have you ever found that single word that does not shame you but honors you? If you haven't, make one up for yourself today — no one will see it, it will be yours alone.
