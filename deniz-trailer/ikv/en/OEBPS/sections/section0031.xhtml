Your Turn

Have you ever experienced a loss you couldn't name — the kind where no one died but something inside you was gone? Do you grieve in silence, or do you cry? What does the sentence "some things do not choose you, this is not your worthlessness" make you feel? This week let's try a small exercise: find a quiet moment, sit down with a piece of paper, and write down, word for word, that whispering guilt sentence of yours — the kind like "you could have done more." Then beneath it, write what you would say in reply if a dear friend told you that sentence. Place the two side by side and see the difference. If there's a bond you want to keep open, write its name at the very bottom too.
