Your Turn

Is there a meaning, a mission, into which your pain could be transformed? Have you ever considered that your deepest wound might be the most genuine hand you can hold out to someone else? Now do a small exercise: write on a piece of paper that sentence you struggled with most — the one that begins "I wish someone had told me this in time." Then, beneath it, write to whom you could say that sentence today. If you have a big dream, add the smallest concrete step you could take toward it this week — a step so small you couldn't find an excuse "not to do it." And finally: what could you delegate to someone else and trust them with a little? Write it down.
