Your Turn

Has there been a period when your life took place in "later," when you suffocated from waiting? Which door did you use to escape — work, alcohol, something else? And perhaps the hardest of all: which chance did you say "no" to in order to protect yourself, and then spend years thinking "what if I had done it?" A small exercise for this week: write a single sentence to your self of back then, in your mature voice of today — not an accusation, but a sentence that says "I understand you." Then set yourself a small, concrete "enough" boundary for this week: where, to what, will you politely say "no"?
