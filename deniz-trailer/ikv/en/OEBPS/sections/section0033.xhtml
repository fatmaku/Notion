Your Turn

Has there ever been a period when you thought "there is something fundamentally wrong with me"? When did that voice first appear — whose voice did it resemble? Do you know yourself through a "judgment" or through an "explanation"? Now do a small exercise: take a trait you have labeled a "flaw" in yourself (for example, "I'm too messy," "I'm too sensitive," "I can't focus") and turn it from a sentence of accusation into a sentence of description. Instead of "I'm bad because…," write "My system works like this:…" Start with a single trait; for three days, rewrite the same trait a little more tenderly each day. You will feel the difference.
