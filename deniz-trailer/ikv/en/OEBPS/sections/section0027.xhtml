Your Turn

Have you ever changed something on the outside — weight, appearance, career, a new phone, a new relationship — and noticed that the void inside stayed right where it was? What do you actually tie your worth to: the scale, the mirror, the gaze of others, the number of likes? Be honest with yourself for a moment. Mini-exercise: One evening this week, step in front of the mirror and write a short letter to that "old child" inside you — to your most fragile, most bashful self. Don't criticize him; tell him what he needs to hear. Then, completely independent of your appearance, simply because "you exist," choose one small kindness for yourself and actually do it.
