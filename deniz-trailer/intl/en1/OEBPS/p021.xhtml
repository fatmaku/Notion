Are You a Super-Brain Kid Too?

Pusula fluttered her wings at the window that evening. Sometimes she would visit other children, sometimes she would appear beside Deniz, and sometimes Deniz would find her voice in a sentence within himself. “But I’m leaving something with you,” she said.

“What?” asked Deniz.

“Your instruction manual. You know it now: your eyes are super, your ears are super, your skin is super. You’ve learned to check your traffic light, to do your charging dance, to announce your balloon. And most important of all: being different doesn’t mean being broken.”

🎈 Game: Find your own super powers! Answer these questions together — there’s no right or wrong, there’s only YOU:

1. Do you sometimes hear sounds that nobody else hears? 👂
2. Are tags and itchy clothes your enemies? 👕
3. Could you eat your favorite food every single day? 🍝
4. Do you jump and flap your hands when you’re happy? 🦘
5. Do you sometimes love one thing SO much that you learn everything about it? 🦖

How many times did you say “yes”? Every “yes” is a super power. And listen: your “no” answers are super too — because every brain is super in its own way.

🦋 “We’re different, not less.”
— Never forget that. Pusula is always with you.
