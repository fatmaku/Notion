The Day We Touched the Clouds

On Friday evening, Deniz was looking at the sky through the window. “Daddy,” he said, “can we touch the clouds?”

His dad was just about to say “We can’t, sweetheart, clouds live up in the sky”… when he stopped. Because at that very moment, Pusula had landed right on the tip of his nose. (Dads can’t see butterflies, but they can feel them.)

His dad thought about it: a cloud is really just millions of tiny water drops floating in the air. Fog is made of tiny water drops too. And the fog on a mountaintop… is a cloud!

“You know what,” said his dad, “we can touch them. This weekend, we’re going up a mountain.”

On Saturday morning, they set off. The road was long. Deniz asked for two breaks, and once he said, “My balloon is puffing up” — so they stopped, and the balloon went down. And at last… they stood on the top of the mountain, right inside the fog.

Deniz opened his hands to the air. The cloud flowed between his fingers. It was cool and wet.

“DADDY!” Deniz shouted. “CLOUDS ARE WET!”

His dad laughed, and that evening he wrote a single sentence in his notebook: “I’m so glad I didn’t say ‘we can’t.’”

🦋 Super-brain kids don’t believe in “impossible.”
They ask, “Why not?”
And sometimes that question carries you all the way to the clouds.

🦋 Pusula saysYour questions are precious. Never, ever be ashamed of asking “Why?” Everything that makes the world beautiful began with somebody asking, “Why not?”
