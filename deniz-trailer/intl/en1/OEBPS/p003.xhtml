Deniz and Pusula the Butterfly

This is the story of Deniz. Deniz is seven years old, and inside his head he carries a super brain.

What’s a super brain, you ask? It’s this: Deniz’s eyes see everything — even the tiniest crumb on the carpet. His ears hear everything — even the refrigerator’s song. Yes, refrigerators sing. If you can’t hear it, your ears are simply a different kind, that’s all.

Deniz’s skin feels a lot, too — the tag in his sweater, the seam in his sock, the temperature of the water. Some children never notice these things; for Deniz, every one of them is huge.

One day, a blue butterfly landed on Deniz’s window. Its wings sparkled and shimmered.

“Hello, Deniz,” said the butterfly. “My name is Pusula. I find children with super brains, and I teach them the instruction manual for their amazing heads.”

Deniz’s eyes grew big and round. “My brain is super? But everyone keeps telling me ‘you’re too loud,’ ‘why won’t you eat,’ ‘sit still’…”

Pusula laughed. When butterflies laugh, their wings tremble — did you know that?

“That’s exactly why I’m here,” said Pusula. “You have super powers, but nobody has ever shown you the settings. Are you ready? The adventure begins!”

🦋 “Being different doesn’t mean being broken.
Being different means coming with a different super power.”

💜 For Parents and TeachersRead this book together; every adventure is a scene from everyday life. If your child asks, “Is that me?”, the loveliest answer is: “It does sound like you, doesn’t it? Tell me — how is it for you?” This book is a conversation starter — not a lesson.
