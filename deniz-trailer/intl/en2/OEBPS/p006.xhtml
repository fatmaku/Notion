ADVENTURE 1

The Pee Bell That Rings Too Late

Deniz was building an enormous city with his toy trains. The tracks ran under the sofa, curved around a mountain of cushions and finally reached the blue station.

His mom called from the doorway: “We’re going out in a minute. Shall we do a toilet check?”

“Nope!” said Deniz. “I don’t need to go at all.”

And that really was how it felt. The train still needed its last bridge. How would the city work if a bridge was missing?

Five minutes later Deniz suddenly pressed his legs together. The bell had gone straight from a whisper to a siren.

“I NEED TO GO NOW!” he shouted.

He ran. But a few drops had already gone into his pants.

Deniz’s face turned bright red. “I’m not a baby,” he whispered.

Pusula landed on the mirror above the sink. “An accident doesn’t make you smaller,” she said. “Your pee bell rings late sometimes. For a late bell, we ask the clock for help.”

“How will the clock know when I need to go?” asked Deniz.

“It won’t,” said Pusula. “It will only remind you to check with your body.”

His mom brought clean clothes. She didn’t tell him off and she didn’t pull a face. “An accident. We’ll clean it up,” she said. “Next time we’ll check before we go out.”
