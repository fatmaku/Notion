ADVENTURE 4

The Toothbrush Dragon

Every evening the toothbrush waited in the cup in the bathroom. The longer Deniz looked at it, the more the bristles looked like dragon’s teeth.

The toothpaste smelled very sharp. The foam filled his mouth, and when the brush touched his gums his whole face tingled.

“Two minutes,” said his mom.

For Deniz, two minutes meant two hours in the dragon’s cave.

One evening he ran away without even putting the brush in his mouth.

Pusula settled on the edge of the mirror. “We do have to protect your teeth,” she said. “But you don’t have to fight with only one brush and only one toothpaste.”

The next day they tried three soft brushes: one with a small head, one with a thick handle and one that didn’t vibrate. They asked the dentist about suitable toothpastes. Deniz chose the one with the milder taste.

Then they changed the order. First Deniz brushed. Afterwards his mom asked, “Is it my turn?” — and gently went over the spots he had missed.

Deniz still didn’t love it. But now the dragon had a door.
