THE ENDING

I Know the Language of My Body

Pusula laid the envelopes of the body post office out in front of Deniz again.

The pee bell. The poop train. Bubble Mountain. The toothbrush dragon. The haircut mission. The night landing strip. The morning stations. The body door.

“Have you solved them all?” she asked.

Deniz shook his head. “No. Sometimes I still notice too late. Sometimes I don’t want a bath. Sometimes sleep doesn’t come.”

Pusula smiled. “Then what have you learned?”

Deniz looked at the envelopes.

“That I’m not bad. My body has a language. If I can’t hear it, I check. If something is hard, I say so. If something hurts, I ask for help. And I guard the door of my body myself.”

Pusula’s wings painted golden paths on the walls of the room.

“That’s the instruction manual,” she said. “Not knowing every answer — but knowing that we look for the answer together.”
