ADVENTURE 7

Not a Morning Race, but Morning Stations

Mornings always took Deniz by surprise. He had only just got out of bed and already somebody was saying, “Come on!”

“Come on, get dressed.”

“Come on, breakfast.”

“Come on, your bag.”

“Come on, your shoes.”

Every “come on” threw another ball at his brain. Deniz didn’t know which one to catch. He would put on one sock, then spot a toy on the floor, then go for a drink of water, then forget why he was standing in the kitchen.

One day the school bus arrived and Deniz had only one shoe on.

“I’m so slow,” he said sadly.

Pusula flew above the door. “You’re not slow. Your stations are getting muddled up.”

That evening the family set up four stations around the house. A dressing basket next to the bed. A care card in the bathroom. The breakfast place in the kitchen. A parking spot for the bag and shoes by the door.

In the mornings the whole house would no longer shout at once. Only the next station would speak.
