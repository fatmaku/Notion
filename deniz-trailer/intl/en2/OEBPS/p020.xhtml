🦋 Pusula says

If a care job feels hard, instead of saying “I won’t do it”, have a look at “What exactly is hard?” Is it the smell, the taste, the touch or the order?

💜 For Parents and Teachers

Toothbrushing is a health need and cannot be left entirely to the child’s choice; but the size of the brush, the handle, the taste of the toothpaste, the order, the use of the mirror and the way an adult helps can all be adapted. Many children need adult supervision and help with brushing for a long time.

If there is pain, bleeding gums, visible decay, bad breath, or if brushing stops working altogether, consult a children’s dentist. Rather than forcing the mouth open, build a step-by-step plan with a dental practice that understands sensory needs.
