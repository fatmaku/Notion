WHAT PUSULA SAYS

The haircut took three sections.

Section one: the sides. Break.

Section two: the back of the neck. Change of T-shirt.

Section three: the top. Done.

After each section Deniz stood up, shook out his shoulders and wiped his neck with the damp cloth.

When he left, his hair wasn’t perfect. One side was a little longer. But Deniz walked out not in tears — he walked out having been heard.

His dad asked, “What shall we change next time?”

Deniz answered right away: “No paper tape. And the back of my neck first.”

Pusula spread her wings. “That’s the mission report!”
