ADVENTURE 5

The Haircut Space Mission

When Deniz saw the barber’s chair, the back of his neck started itching before a single hair had been cut.

The paper around his neck, the plastic cape, the buzzing of the clippers, the cut hair falling onto his skin … each one was its own alarm.

“Only ten minutes,” said the barber.

But ten different things were happening in Deniz’s body at once.

Pusula landed on his shoulder. “We’ve set off on a space mission without preparing,” she said.

This time they didn’t start right away. Deniz first held the clippers while they were switched off. Then he felt the vibration in his hand. The barber cut one strand and stopped. Deniz saw in the mirror what had happened.

His dad opened a bag beside him: a soft cotton cape, ear defenders, a spare T-shirt, a damp cloth and Deniz’s favorite little spaceship.

Deniz chose his own signal. When he raised his hand, everyone would stop.

“Is mission control ready?” asked the barber.

Deniz took a deep breath. “Section one is ready.”
