ADVENTURE 8

The Door of My Body Belongs to Me

After school a distant relative came over and opened his arms wide.

“Come here, let me give you a great big hug!” said the relative.

Deniz stepped back. He didn’t want to be hugged. But the sentence “That would be rude” popped into his head.

When the relative came one step closer, Deniz’s body froze. He could say neither “yes” nor “no”.

Pusula landed on Deniz’s cap. “You’re the guard at the door of your body,” she whispered.

Deniz looked at his mom. She came straight over to him.

“Deniz doesn’t want a hug today,” she said. “He can wave, do a high five, or just say hello.”

Deniz chose to wave.

At home his mom explained two kinds of secrets. Secrets that end in happiness, like a birthday surprise. And secrets that sit heavy inside you, that frighten you, or where somebody says, “Don’t tell anyone.”

“A secret that sits heavy inside you is not kept,” said his mom. “You tell me, your dad, your teacher or another grown-up you trust. You will never get into trouble for it.”
