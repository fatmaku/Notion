🦋 Pusula says

If you don’t want a hug, you can say “no”. And if you freeze, that isn’t your fault either. You can tell a grown-up you trust about it afterwards.

💜 For Parents and Teachers

Bodily autonomy is learned in everyday life: never making a child kiss anyone, stopping tickling the moment “stop” is said, and offering alternative greetings. Teach children the correct names for private parts, the difference between safe secrets and secrets that weigh on them, and help them name more than one trusted adult.

If a child tells you about an uncomfortable touch, listen without interrogating, say that you believe him, stress that it is not his fault, and get professional support. In the US you can contact the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline (1-800-422-4453); in the UK, the NSPCC Helpline (0808 800 5000) or Childline (0800 1111, free and confidential for children). Your school’s safeguarding lead, school counselor or local children’s services can also help.
