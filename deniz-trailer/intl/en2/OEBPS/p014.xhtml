ADVENTURE 3

Bubble Mountain and the Bathroom Map

On Sunday evening his dad said, “Bath time.”

Every brake inside Deniz was pulled at once.

“Now?” said Deniz. “But I’m not ready!”

A bath looked like one single job. For Deniz there were a hundred jobs inside it: stop playing, take off your clothes, check the water temperature, get wet, put up with the smell of the shampoo, keep the bubbles out of your eyes, dry off, put on pajamas.

“This isn’t a bath,” said Deniz. “This is an expedition up Bubble Mountain!”

Pusula landed on a tile. “You don’t climb a mountain without a map,” she said.

His dad drew six simple pictures on a sheet of paper: a parking space for toys, the laundry basket, a hand in the water, washing your body, a towel, pajamas.

Then he gave Deniz two choices: “Blue towel or orange towel? Hair today or tomorrow?”

Deniz chose the orange towel. He checked the water temperature himself with his wrist. Because the noise of the fan bothered him, they switched it off.

Bubble Mountain was still high. But now he could see the path.
