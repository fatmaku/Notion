ADVENTURE 6

The Night Shift of the Brain That Won’t Sleep

Night had come. The house was quiet. But Deniz’s brain was not quiet.

He thought about the picture he had painted that morning. He thought about tomorrow’s trip. He thought about whether dinosaurs dream. Then he thought, “What if I can’t get to sleep?” — and that thought woke up all the other thoughts.

“Close your eyes,” said his mom.

Deniz closed them. The lights in his brain shone even brighter.

Pusula settled on the pillow. “Some brains don’t have an off switch,” she said. “They need a landing strip.”

Together they made a night shift plan. After the exciting game, bed would not come right away. In between there would be a bridge: toilet, teeth, dim light, a short book, parking tomorrow’s questions on a piece of paper — then bed.

Deniz drew his question, “Do dinosaurs dream?”, as a picture on the paper. He put it in the “look it up tomorrow” box.

Once his brain understood that the question wouldn’t be lost, it slowed down a little.
