🦋 Pusula says

If an accident happens, you don’t have to hide. You can say, “There’s been an accident, will you help me?” An accident gets cleaned up — the shame doesn’t stick to you.

💜 For Parents and Teachers

Toilet accidents are usually not on purpose. A picture sequence, clothes that are easy to pull down, an easily reachable toilet, a footstool and regular check-in moments can all help. Never point it out in front of other people; use a private, neutral tone.

Frequently repeated accidents, burning or pain when peeing, fever, a strong smell, peeing very often or very rarely — or new accidents in a child who was previously dry — should be checked by a doctor.
