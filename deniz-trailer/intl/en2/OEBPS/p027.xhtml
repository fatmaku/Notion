WHAT PUSULA SAYS

On the first night the plan didn’t work yet. Deniz turned over again, got up again, asked for water again.

His mom didn’t say, “The plan has failed.” She said, “Your body is still learning.”

On the third night Deniz followed the order himself. Teeth, book, question park, bed.

When he was in bed, Pusula taught him a game: squeeze your toes, let go. Squeeze your legs, let go. Squeeze your hands, let go. Then feel how the bed is carrying you.

Deniz didn’t chase after his thoughts. He let them drift past.

After a while one of the lights on the night shift went out. Then another one.

In the morning his mom asked, “How did you sleep?”

Deniz yawned. “My brain ended the shift without telling me.”
