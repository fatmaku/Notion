WHAT PUSULA SAYS

The first station was getting dressed. The clothes had been in the basket since the night before.

The second station was face and teeth.

The third station was breakfast.

The fourth station was the door: bag, coat, two shoes.

If Deniz moved on to the next station before finishing the one before, his mom didn’t give a long lecture. She simply pointed at the card.

One morning Deniz arrived at the door station and stopped. He looked at both of his shoes. Then he burst out laughing.

“Today you both came along!”

He didn’t have to run for the bus. He walked there in good time.

Pusula landed on his cap. “The morning was never a race,” she said. “It just didn’t have a map.”
