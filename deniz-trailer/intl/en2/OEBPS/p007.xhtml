WHAT PUSULA SAYS

The next day his mom hung three pictures next to the door: shoes, toilet, door.

The order for going out was now: get the shoes ready, do a toilet check, then out through the door.

On the first day Deniz said, “But I don’t need to go.”

“You don’t have to need to,” his mom answered. “This is only the check.”

Deniz went to the toilet, listened to his body — and noticed that he did need to go a little after all.

“Wow,” he said. “I found it before the bell even rang!”

Pusula did a somersault in the air. “That’s called being a body detective.”

For a whole week the bell rang sometimes early, sometimes late. One more accident happened. Nobody said, “Not again?” They cleaned up, made a note of it and carried on.

In the end Deniz found his magic sentence:

“I don’t know if I need to go. I’m going to check.”
