🦋 Pusula says

When sleep is hard, don’t be cross with yourself. Sleep is not a butterfly you catch; it settles by itself in a calm place you have made ready.

💜 For Parents and Teachers

A predictable routine that runs in a similar order every night helps the body understand what comes next. Instead of an abrupt jump from stimulating activity into bed, build a calm bridge. Rather than forcing the child to sleep, make the environment sleep-friendly.

If there is long-lasting sleeplessness, clear difficulty functioning during the day, very loud snoring, pauses in breathing, night-time pain or intense anxiety, speak to your pediatrician.
