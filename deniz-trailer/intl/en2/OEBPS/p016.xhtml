🦋 Pusula says

If a big job looks like a mountain, break it into small steps with pictures. You can ask, “What’s the next step?”

💜 For Parents and Teachers

Daily care is not a single action for a child but the sum of many transitions and sensory experiences. Advance warning, a picture sequence, limited choices, the right water temperature, unscented products and the right to a short break can all reduce resistance.

The goal is not to give up on hygiene, but to adapt the route to the child’s nervous system. Respect the child’s privacy, explain any necessary help beforehand and leave the child the steps he can do himself.
