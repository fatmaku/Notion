💜 For Parents and Teachers

Morning difficulties usually come not from unwillingness but from a combination of sleep inertia, sense of time, working memory and switching between tasks. Laying out clothes the night before, fixed places for belongings, one-step instructions and a visual route work better than verbal pressure.

Measure success not by “he didn’t dawdle at all”, but by his being able to follow the sequence with less and less outside support.
