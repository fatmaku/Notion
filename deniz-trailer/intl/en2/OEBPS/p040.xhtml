GAME

My Own Body Post Office

Together with your child, make five cards with no writing on them:

  • Toilet symbol — “Do I want to check right now?”
  • Water and food symbol — “Does my body need fuel?”
  • Moon/pillow symbol — “Do I need a rest?”
  • Brush/towel symbol — “Which part of getting ready is hard?”
  • Heart-and-door symbol — “Is my body saying yes, no, or I don’t know?”

The child can use a card by speaking, pointing, drawing or simply choosing. The aim is not to find the right answer, but to start a conversation with the body.

“My body is my home. I’m learning its messages and I guard its door.”
