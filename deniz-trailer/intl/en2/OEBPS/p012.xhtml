🦋 Pusula says

If pooping hurts, don’t keep it to yourself. Say, “It hurts me.” Pain is not your fault, and asking for help is nothing to be embarrassed about.

💜 For Parents and Teachers

Holding in stools, hard stools and soiling can be linked to long-standing constipation; the child is not doing it on purpose. Pressure and shaming make the holding worse. A footstool and a calm routine after meals are helpful adjustments to the environment — but the treatment plan should be decided by a pediatrician.

If there are hard or very large stools, pain when passing a stool, blood, a bloated tummy, loss of appetite, several days without a stool or repeated soiling of pants, speak to your pediatrician without delay. Do not start or stop any medication without medical advice.
