WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Deniz found his own rhythm: front teeth, side teeth, back teeth. For each section, one color of the little sand timer ran out.

When there was too much foam, he spat. Instead of pressing hard, he moved the brush slowly.

One evening Pusula asked, “Has the dragon gone?”

Deniz thought about it before he rinsed his mouth. “No,” he said. “But now I know how to talk to him.”

Then he held the brush out to his mom. “Now you can check.”

It never became Deniz’s favorite job. Even so, he learned that body care is sometimes not about liking it, but about finding a safe way to do it.
