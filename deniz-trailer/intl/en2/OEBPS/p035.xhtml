WHAT PUSULA SAYS

His mom explained the private parts of the body to Deniz in an age-appropriate way, using the correct names. She told him that the places covered by a swimming costume are private.

“Sometimes a doctor or a carer helps for health or hygiene reasons,” she said. “But they explain first what they are going to do. When it’s possible, they ask permission. If it feels uncomfortable, you can say so. A grown-up you trust will be there with you.”

Then they played a game. His mom said a situation and Deniz chose the safe sentence:

“Can I have a hug?” — “Not right now, thank you.”

“Don’t tell anyone this secret.” — “I tell people about secrets that sit heavy inside me.”

“Come on, let’s go without telling your mom.” — “I’ll ask a grown-up I trust first.”

Pusula drew a golden door. She gave the key to Deniz.

“The door of your body belongs to you,” she said. “Love never forces a door open.”
