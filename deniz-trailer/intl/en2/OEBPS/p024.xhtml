🦋 Pusula says

Before you go somewhere difficult, it helps to look, to touch, to try out the sound and to choose a break signal. Preparing is not cowardice — it’s clever courage.

💜 For Parents and Teachers

During a haircut the sensory load arrives through many channels at once. Preparation with photos or video, a quiet appointment time, a practice run at home, a cotton cape, spare clothes and a cut divided into sections can all help. Instead of “It’s nothing”, recognize that the child is experiencing five separate things at the same time.

Don’t aim for a salon-perfect result every time. Trust and bodily boundaries are worth more than a symmetrical haircut.
