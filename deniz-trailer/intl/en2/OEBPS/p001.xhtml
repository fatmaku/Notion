A Note for Grown-Ups

This book is not a medical resource; it offers no diagnosis and no treatment. Deniz’s adventures are inspired by the everyday lives of children who notice body signals late, who reach their limits sensorially during care routines, or who need support with transitions.

A toilet accident, holding in a poop, trouble with toothbrushing or difficulty sleeping are not a child’s “disgrace”. Our aim is to give the child a safe language for listening to his body — and to give adults small tools for working together instead of applying pressure. If there is pain, blood, burning when peeing, fever, long-lasting constipation, frequently repeated accidents, marked snoring or pauses in breathing, or any difficulty that seriously affects daily life, please speak to your pediatrician, your dentist or the relevant specialist.

Read the book together. Do not answer in Deniz’s place. Ask, “How is it for you?” — and accept that the answer may be a word, a picture, a gesture or silence.
