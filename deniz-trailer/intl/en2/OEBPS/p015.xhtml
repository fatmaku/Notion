WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Every time a step was finished, Deniz turned the card over.

Toys parked. Card turned.

Clothes in the basket. Card turned.

Water temperature checked. Card turned.

At one point the bubbles came close to his ear. Deniz’s shoulders shot up.

“Break,” said Deniz.

His dad turned off the water. They waited for three breaths. Then they carried on, when Deniz was ready.

When the bath was over, Deniz wrapped himself in the orange towel. “I climbed the mountain myself,” he said.

Pusula smiled. “Because you could see where the path went.”
