ADVENTURE 7

Before the Answer in My Head Escapes

In science the teacher asked, “Why do bees visit flowers?”

Deniz knew the answer. Bees, pollen and bright flowers went flying through his mind.

He forgot to put his hand up.

“For honey!” Deniz called out.

“Don’t interrupt,” said the teacher.

Deniz went quiet. At the next question he raised his hand. This time the teacher chose someone else. Deniz felt the answer about to escape, and he spoke out again.

Pusula left a small “park my idea” card on his desk. Deniz drew one single word from his answer on the card: flower.

He put his hand up. While he waited, he looked at his card. The answer wasn’t escaping any more.

When his turn came he said, “While the bee collects nectar, it carries pollen too.”

The teacher smiled. “How did you keep hold of your idea this time?”

Deniz held up the card.
