💜 For Parents and Teachers

Make time concrete with more than words: a visual timer, an hourglass, a piece of music or a finishing card. Instead of “stop right now”, give the child a chance to close the activity safely. When leaving a special interest is hard, an “I’ll carry on” folder, a photo, or showing the next time on the calendar can help a great deal.

A transition warning is not a negotiation; it is preparation time for the nervous system. The boundary can stay exactly the same — the way to it just becomes more visible.
