💜 For Parents and Teachers

Do not read frequent interrupting only as rudeness. Impulse control, working memory and waiting skills may all be under strain at once. Teach the expected behavior in advance, briefly and visually; do not only correct it in the moment it goes wrong.

A few seconds of thinking time before answers, jotting a note on a small card, sharing with a partner, and offering other ways to take part will let far more children be heard.
