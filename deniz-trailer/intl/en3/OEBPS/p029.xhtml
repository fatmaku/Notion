💜 For Parents and Teachers

Long copying from the board may not measure what a child knows; it may only measure visual-motor speed. Talk through options with the school team: printed notes, less copying, extra time, a keyboard, spoken answers, or speech-to-text support.

Do not brush aside a child’s hand pain or heavy tiredness. If difficulties with motor skills, vision or learning continue, ask for a proper assessment. The aim is not to take writing away from the child, but to stop their knowledge staying locked up while they are still learning to write.
