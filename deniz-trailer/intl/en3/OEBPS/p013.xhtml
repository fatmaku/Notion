💜 For Parents and Teachers

Not being able to start is not always defiance. The task may be too big, too vague, too dull, or it may stir up a fear of getting it wrong. Shrink the first step until the child can manage it, and work calmly beside them for a few minutes. You could call this “body company”: the grown-up does not do the work instead of the child; they are simply a presence that carries the starting.

Say “let’s start together for two minutes” instead of “finish it all”. For longer homework, use short work-and-rest cycles and a visible finish line.
