THE ENDING

My Way Is a Way Too

At the end of the school year the teacher asked the class, “What have you learned about school this year?”

One child said, “My times tables.”

Another said, “The layers of the Earth.”

Deniz thought about it.

“I’ve learned how to find my way,” he said.

The class looked at him.

Deniz went on: “If I don’t understand a task, I ask for the first step. If I can’t see time, I use a timer. If my idea won’t fit into my pencil, I say it out loud. If someone hurts me, I go to a grown-up I trust.”

Pusula opened her wings in front of the window.

School City was still big. On some days the paths still got tangled. Some tasks were still hard.

But now, before Deniz said “I can’t do it”, he asked himself a different question:

“Which way does my brain take to get to this one?”

“My way may be different — it still arrives at learning.”
