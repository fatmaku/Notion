WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Knowing and Being Able to Write It Down Can Be Two Different Skills

Pusula laid three ways of telling in front of Deniz:

  • Writing it
  • Saying it
  • Drawing it and then explaining it

“But don’t you have to write at school?” asked Deniz.

“You’ll keep on learning to write,” said Pusula. “And at the same time you can have a bridge that shows what you know.”
