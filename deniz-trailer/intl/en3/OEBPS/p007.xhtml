ADVENTURE 1

The Invisible Map of the First Day

On Monday the class teacher said, “Get your exercise books out and start the day.”

Zips opened all around the room. Pencils came out onto the tables. Chairs scraped.

Deniz stayed where he was.

Which movement was “starting the day”? The math book? The English book? Was he supposed to write the date? Or listen to the teacher?

The child next to him was already writing. Deniz felt his cheeks go warm.

When the teacher said, “Come on, Deniz, we’re waiting for you,” the path in his head got even more tangled.

Pusula popped her head out from behind the pencil case.

“When one sentence has lots of doors in it, you’re allowed to ask about the first door,” she said.

Deniz put his hand up.

“Please could you tell me the first thing to do?”

The teacher paused. She drew three small pictures on the board: blue book, date, a listening ear.

Deniz took out the blue book. The first stone of the path was in place.
