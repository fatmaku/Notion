My School Wayfinding Bag

The child, the parents and the teacher make five small cards together:

  • First-step card: A footprint — “What is the tiniest first step?”
  • Time card: An hourglass — “I want to see how much time is left.”
  • Idea card: A lightbulb and a parking space — “I’ll park my idea here.”
  • Break card: A quiet corner — “I need a short break to sort myself out.”
  • Safety card: Three grown-ups — “It didn’t stop. I need help.”

The cards are not introduced in the middle of a telling-off, but at a calm moment, and tried out in role play. If the child can speak, they say the sentence; if speaking feels hard, they show the card. The teacher knows in advance what each card means.

💜 A closing question for grown-ups

“Does the child need to try harder — or does the way need to become more visible?”
