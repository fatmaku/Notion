ADVENTURE 4

The Time Bubble

At recess Deniz was drawing a spaceship.

The teacher said, “We’re going inside in five minutes.”

Deniz nodded. Five minutes felt like a long road to him.

He drew the ship’s wing. He added flames to the engine. He made a new planet.

Then the bell rang.

“But I’ve only just started!” Deniz shouted.

Everyone else was already in line. His five minutes had gone “pop” like a bubble.

Pusula brought an hourglass. At first the top part was full. As the sand ran, the time that was left could be seen.

Two minutes before the end, the teacher showed Deniz a finishing card.

Deniz drew one last window on the ship and put the paper in his “I’ll carry on” folder.

The bell rang again. This time, time did not shove him from behind; he walked across the bridge in front of him.
