💜 For Parents and Teachers

Instead of a long telling-off for every forgotten item, make the system visible. Give things a fixed home, use color coding, a bag with few compartments and a short check picture by the door. Plan a weekly “bag reset” time.

Packing the bag for the child every time solves the problem in the short run, but it can leave the skill invisible. Fade the support in stages: first together, then with the picture only, and finally an independent try.
