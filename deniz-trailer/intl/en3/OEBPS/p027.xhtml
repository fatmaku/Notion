ADVENTURE 6

The Hand Whose Pencil Gets Tired

The teacher wrote a long sentence on the board. Deniz understood it. In fact, he had an even better sentence in his head.

But the moment his pencil touched the paper, his hand slowed down.

Fitting the letters onto the line, spacing the words, putting the period in the right place... each one was a separate job. After a while his fingers ached.

The board was about to be wiped. Deniz was only halfway.

“I didn’t finish in time,” he said — and nobody heard the lovely sentence in his head.

Pusula pointed to the little voice-recording button on the teacher’s desk.

“You can tell me your idea first,” said the teacher. “Then you write your short part.” She also gave him a printed copy of the board.

Deniz said the sentence that had been in his head. His classmates turned round to listen.

His pencil might be slow, but his idea was quick and bright.
