WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Not Knowing the Way Is Not the Same as Not Being Able

Pusula unfolded a small map of the school. It had only five pictures on it:

  • The door
  • The coat pegs
  • The classroom
  • The toilet
  • A grown-up I trust

“You don’t have to learn the whole school in one day,” said Pusula. “You can learn the way you need today.”

Deniz drew a little star next to the picture of his teacher.

“What’s that?” asked Pusula.

“The person I go to if I get lost,” said Deniz.

“That is exactly what school courage is,” said Pusula. “Not knowing everything on your own — but knowing who to turn to.”
