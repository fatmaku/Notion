WHAT PUSULA SAYS

To Remember, Put a Sign Outside Your Head

“Sometimes your brain keeps the information but doesn’t open the door at the right moment,” said Pusula. “Signs are the doorbell.”

Deniz did not write three rules; he used three pictures:

  • Paper that comes home goes straight into the clear folder.
  • Finished homework goes straight into the bag.
  • At night the bag goes straight beside the door.
