💜 For Parents and Teachers

Never let reading aloud become a source of shame or a speed race. Try supports such as preparation time, a shorter section, a line window, clear and generously spaced print, following along with an audiobook, and extra time. Look at the child’s decoding skill and their comprehension as two separate things.

Where the difficulty persists, do not stop at “try harder” — this may be dyslexia. Start the school’s support process (SEN support, an IEP or 504 plan, depending on your system) and ask for assessment by an appropriate specialist. Reasonable adjustments and accommodations are not a privilege; they are the way in to learning.
