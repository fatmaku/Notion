💜 For Parents and Teachers

School rules are clear to grown-ups and often invisible to children. Before the first days, or when a child moves to a new class, prepare a short walk-through with photos, a simple picture of how the day runs, and a card showing “people you can ask for help”. Instead of “you know that already”, show it once, practice it together, then slowly fade the prompt.

If a child is very frightened of school, is left out over a long period, or complains of tummy aches and headaches, do not read it as simple reluctance. Talk with the child and the school early, in concrete terms and without blame.
