💜 For Parents and Teachers

Instead of wide instructions like “get ready”, “do it properly” or “pull yourself together”, name one step that can be seen: “Put the blue book on the table.” Check understanding not with “Did you understand?” but with “What’s the first step?”

Use a three-part visual sequence, always in the same place on the board: now — next — done. This support does not only help Deniz; it often helps the whole class.
