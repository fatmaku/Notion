WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Asking for Help Is Not Telling Tales

“Telling in order to get somebody into trouble is one thing,” said Pusula. “Telling in order to keep someone safe is quite another.”

Deniz broke his account into four parts:

  • Who?
  • What did they do?
  • Where and when?
  • What do I need now?

“They took my hat. It happened at break. They didn’t stop. I need a grown-up to come with me.”
