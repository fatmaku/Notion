ADVENTURE 3

The Lost Planets in the Backpack

The next morning the teacher said, “Take out your trip permission slips.”

Deniz’s stomach jumped. He had held the slip in his hand yesterday. Then he had put his water bottle in. Then something had happened... but what?

He opened his backpack. Inside was a small universe.

A sock planet. Two crumpled paper asteroids. A pencil rocket with no lid. Half a sandwich moon left over from last week.

There was no permission slip.

A knot tightened in Deniz’s throat. “I always forget,” he said.

“You are not your forgetting,” Pusula answered. “You just haven’t built the place where your things come home to yet.”

Together they made three stations inside the backpack: a clear folder for school papers, a box for pencils, a separate section for food.

By the door they hung a small leaving picture: folder, bottle, bag.

The next day the permission slip came straight out of the clear folder.
