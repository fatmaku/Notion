ADVENTURE 5

The Dancing Letters

At reading time it was Deniz’s turn.

The lines in the book were straight. But when Deniz started to read, the letters did not want to turn into sounds. The “b” and the “d” looked so alike that it took him a moment to tell which sound belonged to which. Long words looked like trains with their carriages muddled up.

Deniz stopped at the very first word.

Someone at the back giggled.

Deniz wanted to close the book. “I can’t read,” he thought.

Pusula laid a small reading window over the page. Only one line showed through.

The teacher said, “If you like, look it over quietly first. Then you can read just two sentences.”

Deniz followed the line with his finger. He read the first sentence slowly. On the second one he got stuck.

“Let’s break this word up together,” said the teacher.

Once the word was in small pieces, the carriages lined up again.
