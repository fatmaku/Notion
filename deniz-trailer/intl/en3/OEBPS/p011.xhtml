ADVENTURE 2

The Start Button and the One-centimeter Step

Deniz came home. There was a worksheet on the table.

The sheet had ten questions on it. But Deniz did not see ten questions — he saw a mountain reaching up over his head.

He picked up his pencil. Put it down. Drank some water. Straightened his hat. Looked for his eraser. Stared out of the window.

His dad said, “Come on, just start.”

Deniz wanted to start too. But the start button was hiding.

Pusula landed on the table and covered most of the sheet with a blank page. Only the name line was showing.

“Don’t look at the mountain — look at the one centimeter in front of your shoe,” she said.

Deniz wrote his name.

Pusula slid the paper down a little. Only the first line of the first question appeared.

Deniz read it.

“Have I started now?”

“Yes,” said Pusula. “Starting isn’t always finishing. Sometimes it’s just the first move anyone can see.”
