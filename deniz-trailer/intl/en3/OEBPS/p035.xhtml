ADVENTURE 8

The Bullying Alarm and the Safe Three

At recess three children took Deniz’s hat.

“Give it back,” said Deniz.

One of them threw the hat to another. “We’re only joking.”

Deniz did not laugh. His tummy went tight. When he tried to get his hat back, they copied the way he moved.

A grown-up called from a distance, “Play nicely, everyone.” But the hat still wasn’t Deniz’s.

“Maybe I’m making too much of it,” Deniz thought.

Pusula spoke in a serious voice. “If it keeps happening, if it hurts you, and if it doesn’t stop when you say stop — then you are allowed to sound the help alarm. That is not telling tales.”

Deniz remembered his safe three: his class teacher, the playground supervisor and the school counselor.

He went to the playground supervisor. “They’re taking my hat and throwing it. I said stop and they didn’t stop. Will you come with me?”

The teacher went with him, got the hat back, and wrote down what had happened. Over the next few days she checked in again.

Deniz saw that he had not been left on his own.
