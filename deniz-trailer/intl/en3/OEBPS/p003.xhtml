GETTING TO KNOW

The First Morning in School City

Deniz stopped at the school gate. Inside, bells were ringing, shoes were running, doors were opening and closing.

His mom said, “You can go to your classroom now.”

But the word “can” opened a huge empty space in Deniz’s head.

Which door? Where does the bag go? When does the coat come off? What do you say to the teacher? Where is the toilet? And what if the bell rings?

The other children walked as if they were carrying an invisible map. Deniz was carrying only his backpack.

Right then a blue butterfly landed on his orange hat.

“I’m lost,” Deniz whispered.

Pusula opened her wings. “Being lost is not the same as failing. Maybe your map just isn’t visible yet.”

“Where do I find the map?” asked Deniz.

“By asking, by looking, and by leaving yourself small signs,” said Pusula.

Pusula flew ahead. Deniz followed her. School City was still big — but now he had his first sign.
