WHAT PUSULA SAYS

A Clear Instruction Is an Open Door

Pusula said, “For your brain to understand a task, it wants to know three things.”

  • What am I doing?
  • What am I doing it with?
  • How will I know I’m finished?

Deniz looked at the three pictures on the board.

“I take out the book. I write the date. Then I stop and listen.”

“Yes,” said Pusula. “Now the way is visible.”
