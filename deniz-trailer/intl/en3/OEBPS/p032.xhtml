WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Open a Channel for the Wish to Join In

“Holding on to an answer that pops into your head can be hard,” said Pusula. “That does not mean you have nothing to say.”

Deniz and his teacher agreed on three ways:

  • Put your hand up.
  • Write or draw one single word on the card.
  • If it is really important, show the silent “help” sign.

The teacher also began giving the whole class thinking time on some questions, then a share-with-your-partner stage, then saying it to the class.
