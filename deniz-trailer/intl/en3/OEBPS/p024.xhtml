WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Reading Is Not an Intelligence Race

“If working out one word takes a lot of energy, there may be less energy left for understanding the story,” said Pusula.

Deniz listened to the same story first. Then he followed the text with wider spaces between the lines. He noticed that he understood the story very well indeed.

“So the story was in my brain all along,” said Deniz.

“Of course it was,” said Pusula. “You just needed a different way to reach the door.”
