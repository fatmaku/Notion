A Note for Grown-Ups

This book does not claim that all children learn the same way. Some children learn more easily by listening, some by seeing, some by moving — and many through a mix of all three.

While the child is listening to the story, they may:

  • point to a moment that happened to them too,
  • try out the short sentence when they feel ready,
  • choose a symbol if they would rather not speak,
  • read one adventure again and again.

Three questions for grown-ups:

  • What can the child not do yet?
  • Which part of the task is invisible or too big?
  • Which small change in the surroundings would raise the chance of success?

Important note: If there are lasting concerns about learning, attention, language, vision, hearing, motor skills or safety at school, the family, the teachers and a suitable specialist should look at it together. This book does not diagnose; it strengthens the child’s voice.
