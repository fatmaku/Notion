💜 For Parents and Teachers

If you do not know how long it will be, do not offer made-up numbers like “two minutes”. Say, “I don’t know the exact time; I’ll go and ask again in ten minutes” — and then keep your word. Make the start, the check-in points and the end of a wait visible.

In waiting places that are sensorily hard, ask about the choice of where to sit, waiting outside, headphones or a movement break. A prepared waiting bag is not a privilege; it is a way in.
