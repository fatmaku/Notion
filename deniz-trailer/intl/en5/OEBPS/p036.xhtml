WHAT PUSULA SAYS

A Good Request for Help Has Three Parts

Pusula broke the help sentence up:

  • The job: What am I doing?
  • Where I’m stuck: Which part is hard?
  • The help I want: Show me, start with me, remind me, or give me a short break.

Deniz came up with a few examples:

“I’m reading the question; I didn’t understand the first word; could you say it another way?” “I’m tidying my room; I don’t know where to start; will you choose the first card?”
