💜 For Parents and Teachers

The feeling of being turned down can arrive very fast and very big. Instead of “You’re overreacting”, keep the boundary short and name the relationship separately: “We can’t go to the park now. I love you; I can see you’re sad.” Saying that does not remove the boundary.

Once the child is calm, sort out together what actually happened: a request was turned down, an idea was postponed, a behavior was limited, or the relationship really does need repairing. You do not have to hand over another treat after every “no”; clear information and a bond that continues are worth more.
