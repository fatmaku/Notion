ADVENTURE 3

The Picture That Wasn’t Perfect

Deniz was drawing a butterfly. The right wing came out exactly as he wanted. The left wing stayed a bit too small.

He used the eraser. The paper went thin.

He drew it again. The line came out crooked.

A voice inside Deniz shouted, “Wrong! Start again!”

He was about to screw the paper up.

Pusula drew a tiny, lopsided star in the corner of the page.

“That star’s gone wrong,” said Deniz.

“That star is a trying star,” said Pusula. “Its job isn’t to be beautiful. Its job is to teach you the next move.”

Together they opened three doors: fix it, work it into the design, or leave it as it is.

Deniz added a small line of wind beside the smaller wing. The butterfly looked as though it were turning.

The picture was not flawless. But it was still Deniz’s picture, and it was finished.
