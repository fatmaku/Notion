ADVENTURE 4

The Long Minutes in the Waiting Room

Deniz went with his mom to an appointment. The receptionist said, “There’ll be a bit of a wait.”

How many minutes was “a bit”?

The light above the chair was buzzing. The wall clock went tick, tick. His mom looked at her phone. Every time the door opened, Deniz stood up.

Five minutes felt like fifty.

“When?” Deniz asked.

“Soon,” said his mom.

Soon did not come.

Pusula divided the waiting into three boxes: what we know, what we don’t know, and what we’re doing now.

What we know: Our turn will come. What we don’t know: The exact time. Now: One thing chosen out of the quiet bag.

Deniz put his headphones on and took out his little drawing pad. Every five minutes his mom would go and check how much longer it would be.

The waiting was not lovely. But it had stopped being a shapeless fog.
