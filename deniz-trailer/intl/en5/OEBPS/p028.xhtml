WHAT PUSULA SAYS

“No” Tells You Something — It Doesn’t Say Who You Are

Pusula asked three questions:

  • What exactly was said no to?
  • Can the reason be explained?
  • Is there another time, another way, or a repair?

“What if it still hurts?” said Deniz.

“It may hurt,” said Pusula. “You give your body time for the feeling to pass. Then you check what it meant.”
