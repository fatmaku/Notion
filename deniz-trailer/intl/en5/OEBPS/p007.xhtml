ADVENTURE 1

Plan Changed! The Crossing Bridge

There was going to be an art lesson at school. Deniz had put his paint box in his bag that morning.

The teacher came into the room. “The art teacher can’t come in today. We’re doing PE instead.”

The class cheered. Deniz did not.

He had already got everything ready in his mind: the smell of the paints, the space picture he was going to make, the order of things on the table. Now everyone was standing up and chairs were scraping back fast.

Deniz covered his ears. “No! It’s art time!”

Pusula left three pictures on his desk: a gentle line through the paintbrush, a trainer, and ten minutes of drawing at the end of the day.

“You’re sad that art was canceled,” said the teacher. “Right now it’s PE. Your paint box is safe. In the last lesson I’ll keep ten minutes for drawing.”

Deniz did not cheer up right away. First he did three strong wall pushes in the corridor. Then he put his sneakers on.

The bridge had not rubbed the feeling out. It had given him somewhere to cross.
