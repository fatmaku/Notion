WHAT PUSULA SAYS

A Boundary and Help Crossing It Can Go Together

Pusula split the screen plan into five parts:

  • Agree it before the time starts.
  • Make the time that is left visible.
  • Choose a safe stopping point.
  • Save, and mark where you’ll come back to.
  • Show what the next activity is beforehand.

“What if I never want to stop?” said Deniz.

“Not wanting to is real,” said Pusula. “The boundary is real too. The bridge helps you walk between them.”
