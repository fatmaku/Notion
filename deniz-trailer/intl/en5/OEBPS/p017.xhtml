💜 For Parents and Teachers

Praising only how good the result looks can raise the pressure to be perfect. Notice the strategy instead: “You worked the crooked line into the design”, “When it got hard you tried another way”, “You left a try that was finished.”

Make the measure of success concrete from the start. For example: “Write three sentences; it doesn’t have to be flawless.” Grown-ups can repair their own small mistakes openly too. When mistakes are safe, the brain keeps on trying.
