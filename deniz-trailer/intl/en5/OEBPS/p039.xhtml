THE ENDING

My Bridge Bag Is With Me

The following Saturday, Deniz made a plan for the park again.

This time the weather was clear. But just as they were leaving, the car had a flat tire.

The old storm rose up inside Deniz.

“The plan’s changed,” he said. His voice was shaking.

Pusula waited quietly beside him.

Deniz opened his bridge bag.

What was the old plan? The park. Why did it change? The car isn’t safe. What’s the new plan? A walk in the gardens nearby while the tire is fixed; the park moved to the afternoon. What’s the first step? Putting my shoes on.

Deniz was still sad. He wanted to go to the park right now.

But between his feeling and the new road there was a bridge.

“So do you like changes now?” Pusula asked.

“No,” said Deniz. “But when a change comes, I lose myself less.”

“I can’t stop the storm — but I can carry my bridge with me.”
