WHAT PUSULA SAYS

A Mistake Is Not Where the Work Ends

Pusula drew a “trying ladder”:

  • Begin.
  • Notice a mistake.
  • Fix it, change it, or leave it.
  • Carry on with what you have learned.

“But what if it doesn’t come out nice?” said Deniz.

“Not every job has the same aim,” said Pusula. “Sometimes it’s to learn, sometimes to tell someone something, sometimes to finish, and sometimes just to enjoy it.”
