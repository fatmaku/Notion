💜 For Parents and Teachers

Using screens only as a reward or a punishment can load the crossing even more heavily. Look at the content, the length, its job in the day and the crossing skill as separate things. Do not belittle a child’s special interest; be curious about what he is actually making in the game.

Use a warning you can be trusted on and a closing routine. Taking the device away suddenly should only be considered where safety genuinely requires it. If sleep, movement, school, relationships and everyday responsibilities are clearly affected over a long period, ask for a professional assessment together with the child; the number of minutes on its own does not tell the whole story.
