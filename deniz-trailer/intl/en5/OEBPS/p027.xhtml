ADVENTURE 6

When “No” Makes Your Heart Ache

Deniz showed his teacher his picture of a spaceship.

“Shall we put it up on the class board?” he asked.

“There’s no room on the board this week,” said the teacher. “We could look again next week.”

Deniz’s ears heard only two words: “No. No room.”

It felt as though a small stone had dropped into the middle of his heart.

“She didn’t like my picture,” he thought. He stuffed the paper into his bag and did not look at his teacher all day.

Pusula picked the heart stone up. “Sometimes a no comes to the thing you asked for — not to the whole of you.”

Deniz turned back to his teacher. “My mind is telling me you didn’t like my picture. Is that right?”

The teacher looked surprised. “No — I looked at your picture very carefully. There’s other work on the board this week. If you like, we’ll put your name on the list for next Friday.”

Deniz did not feel lighter right away. But a line had been drawn between “no room” and “you’re worth nothing”.
