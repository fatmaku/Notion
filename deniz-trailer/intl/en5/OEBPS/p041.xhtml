My Change Bridge

Keep six wordless cards in a folder:

  • Old plan: An arrow pointing back
  • New plan: An arrow pointing forward
  • First step: A single footprint
  • Break: A small safe island
  • Parking space: A box of waiting ideas
  • Help lantern: A hand holding up a light

Choose one small change: a different cup, whose turn it is in a game, or another route home. Let the child lay out the old plan and the new plan with the cards. Then let him choose the first step. If it becomes too much, the rehearsal can be stopped; the aim is not to make a child like change by force.

💜 A closing question for grown-ups

“While I was holding the boundary, did I also show the child the bridge he could cross?”
