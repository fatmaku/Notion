ADVENTURE 7

Two Wishes, One Brain

Deniz wanted to tidy his room. At the same time he wanted to finish the book he had half read, complete his train track and have a drink of water.

First he picked up the book. The train caught his eye. While he was straightening the track he remembered he was thirsty. On his way to the kitchen he spotted a sock on the floor. He picked the sock up and forgot where he was taking it.

Ten minutes later the room was in an even bigger muddle.

“You’re not finishing any of it,” said his dad.

Deniz sat down on the floor. “They’re all shouting at the same time.”

Pusula drew the jobs on small pieces of paper and put them all in a parking space. She took just one card into the “now” box: have a drink of water.

Then the second card: put the socks in the basket.

The train and the book had not disappeared. They were waiting in the “later” place.

For the first time, Deniz heard a single voice in his brain.
