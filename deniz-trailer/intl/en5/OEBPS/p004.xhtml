WHAT PUSULA SAYS

A Change Makes Sense in Three Pieces

Pusula divided a card into three.

  • Old plan: The park.
  • Why it changed: Rain, and staying safe.
  • New plan: Build a den indoors; put the park on the calendar for another day.

“Do I have to like the new plan?” Deniz asked.

“No,” said Pusula. “You can be sad and still cross over to the new plan.”

Deniz did not throw the park picture away. He moved it along to Sunday. The lost thing might not come back — but he had found it a place inside the not-knowing.
