ADVENTURE 5

Screen City and the Bridge Back

Deniz was building a city on his tablet. He was just about to lay the last piece of the bridge.

“Time’s up, switch it off,” said his dad.

Deniz had heard him. But half his brain was still in the city.

“One minute,” said Deniz.

Within that minute the bridge was finished; then a new mission opened up. Deniz tapped that too.

When his dad took the tablet out of his hands, something inside Deniz felt torn. “No!” he shouted, and reached for it.

Pusula drew a bridge back along the edge of the screen.

The next day they built the plan from the beginning: ten minutes, with a two-minute warning; one single action to finish; a picture for saving; carrying the device to its charging home; and a body activity, chosen in advance, for afterwards.

When the time was up, Deniz laid the last stone of the bridge, saved the game and took a screenshot.

“Carry on from here tomorrow,” he said. Then he put the tablet on the charger himself.

The crossing was still hard — but it was not a cliff edge.
