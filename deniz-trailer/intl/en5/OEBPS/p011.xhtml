ADVENTURE 2

The Case of the Missing Red Pencil

Deniz wanted to draw a rocket with his red pencil. He opened his pencil case. There was no red pencil.

He looked on the table. He looked in his bag. He looked on the floor.

His heart started to race.

“Someone’s taken it!” said Deniz. “I’m always losing my things!”

He tipped the pencil case out. He pulled his notebooks onto the floor. The harder he looked, the less his eyes seemed to see.

Pusula blew a detective’s whistle. “Stop the searching storm. Let’s open the case file in order.”

Place one: The last table you used. Place two: The pencil’s home. Place three: Pockets and floor along the way. Step four: Ask one person.

Deniz checked the art table first. Nothing. He opened the narrow side pocket of his pencil case.

The red pencil was there.

Deniz took a deep breath. “My eyes had looked, but my brain hadn’t seen.”
