GETTING TO KNOW

The Island Whose Map Changes Every Day

There was a calendar in Deniz’s room. Across Saturday he had drawn an enormous picture of the park.

On Saturday morning it started to rain.

“The park’s off,” said his dad. “We’re staying in today.”

Deniz looked at the calendar. Then at the window. Then back at the calendar.

“But there’s the park,” said Deniz.

“It’s raining,” said his dad.

“But there’s the park!”

It was as if the road in front of him had dropped into the sea. He didn’t know where to put his foot.

Pusula came flying in between the raindrops. “Welcome to the Island of Changes,” she said.

Deniz frowned. “I didn’t want to come.”

“I know,” said Pusula. “We mostly arrive here without buying a ticket. But we can pack you a bridge bag to carry with you.”
