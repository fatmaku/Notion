WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Line the Jobs Up Outside Your Head, Not Inside It

Pusula made a board with three sections:

  • Now: One small job only.
  • Later: Two jobs at most.
  • Parking space: Good ideas and things for another time.

After each finished job, Deniz chose the next card himself.

“What if I pick the wrong order?” asked Deniz.

“If there’s no safety or time limit, you can try a different order,” said Pusula. “The plan isn’t there to run you; it’s there to line the voices up.”
