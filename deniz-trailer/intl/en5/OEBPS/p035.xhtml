ADVENTURE 8

Asking for Help Is a Superpower

The class was getting ready for a trip. Deniz had three jobs: put his coat on, collect his permission slip, and line up.

His coat zip jammed. The class began to move.

Deniz pulled the zip. He pulled it again. He pulled harder. His hands were shaking.

“I have to do it myself,” he thought.

When everyone else had gone to the door, his eyes filled up. The zip still wasn’t done.

Pusula lit the help lantern. “Doing it on your own and being independent are not the same thing.”

“What do you mean?” asked Deniz.

“Being independent means being able to find the tool or the person you need,” said Pusula.

Deniz went to his teacher. “My zip’s stuck. Could you show me once, and then I’ll try?”

The teacher eased the fabric out of the zip. Deniz pulled it up himself and joined the line.

The help had not taken the job away from him. It had unlocked the way through.
