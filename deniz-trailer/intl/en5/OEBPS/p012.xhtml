WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Searching Is a Plan Too

“The lost thing may be small,” said Pusula, “but the alarm in your body can be big.”

Deniz’s search plan:

  • Stop your hands and breathe out once.
  • Say the name of the thing and where it was last seen.
  • Check the same place carefully once, not quickly ten times.
  • If you can’t find it, ask for help and choose a stand-in plan.

The stand-in plan might be using another pencil, or photographing the work, or looking again later.
