WHAT PUSULA SAYS

News First, Then the Bridge, Then a Choice

Pusula put three tools into the change bag:

  • News: Say what has changed, briefly and honestly.
  • Bridge: Show the very first move between the old and the new.
  • Choice: Offer two options inside one small part of the new plan.

“What if there’s no time to warn me?” said Deniz.

“Then the grown-up talks less, keeps you safe, and shows you the first solid step,” said Pusula.
