💜 For Parents and Teachers

When a child asks for help, do not take the whole job over. Give as much support as is needed: a hint, a model, the first step, a tool, or a short start together. Then leave room for the child to carry on.

Shaming responses like “You can’t even do that?” close the door on asking for help. See a request for help as a skill in self-awareness and communication. If the child cannot say it out loud, he should be able to use a help card, a color or a sign.
