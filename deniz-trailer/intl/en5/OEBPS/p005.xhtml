💜 For Parents and Teachers

Instead of “What’s the big deal?”, name the part that has changed, plainly. Where you can, show the old plan, the reason and the new plan visually. Do not make false promises about something that cannot be replaced; make room for the sadness.

A child does not need unlimited control. Offer one small choice: “In the new plan, den first or a warm drink first?” The choice does not undo the change; it gives the child a branch to hold on to while he crosses.
