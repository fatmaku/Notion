💜 For Parents and Teachers

Do not bring the change card out only in a crisis; rehearse it with small, safe changes. “Plan A / Plan B” language can help. Deliberately springing surprises every day, though, tends to build mistrust rather than tolerance.

If the child has already overflowed, cut back on long explanations and persuading. Short information, a visual new plan, help with settling the body and time to do it all work better. Once he is calm, look together at what actually helped.
