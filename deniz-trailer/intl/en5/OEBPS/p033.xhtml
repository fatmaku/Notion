💜 For Parents and Teachers

Several instructions at once can overload working memory. Give one step, or use a picture card. Stay beside the child as he starts; then step back. Instead of “tidy your room”, define the area and the finish: “Put the socks on the floor into the basket; bring me the card when you’re done.”

Use a “parking space” sheet for the new ideas that pull his attention away. Storing an idea safely, rather than dismissing it, can make it easier to come back to the job in hand.
