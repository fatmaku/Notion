WHAT PUSULA SAYS

You Are Always Allowed to Check the Meaning

Pusula taught three checking sentences:

  • “Is that real, or a joke?”
  • “What did you mean by that?”
  • “I know it was a joke, but I didn’t like it.”

Deniz asked, “Do I have to laugh at a joke?”

“No,” said Pusula. “The point of a joke is to enjoy it together. If only one person is enjoying it and the other one is being made smaller, it needs to stop.”
