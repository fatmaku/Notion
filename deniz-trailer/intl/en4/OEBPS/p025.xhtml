💜 For Parents and Teachers

Do not push a child to be a “good loser” at the most intense moment of the game. Help the body settle first. Teach the loser to ask for a break safely, just as carefully as you teach everyone to congratulate the winner.

Practice with short, low-stakes games at a calm time. Alongside games whose outcome is unknown, make room for cooperative games too. The goal is not for the child never to be upset; it is for him to stay with the feeling without doing harm.
