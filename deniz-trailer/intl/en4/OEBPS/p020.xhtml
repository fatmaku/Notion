WHAT PUSULA SAYS

In a Shared Game There Are Two Brains

Pusula showed three play areas:

  • My area: My own set-up, with my own things.
  • Your area: My friend’s choice, with my friend’s things.
  • Shared area: Decide by taking turns, by drawing lots, or by combining the two ideas.

Deniz said, “Do I have to like every change?”

“No,” said Pusula. “But in a shared game you do need to see where the other brain fits.”
