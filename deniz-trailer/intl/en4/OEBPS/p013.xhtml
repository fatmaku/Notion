💜 For Parents and Teachers

“Go and make friends” is far too wide a task. At a calm moment, act out watching, choosing the right moment, a short opening sentence and the back-up plan. Rather than always placing the child inside a game an adult has set up, build small groups around shared interests.

Do not leave repeated exclusion filed away as the child’s own social skills gap. Look at the group dynamic and the school environment as well; adult support may be needed. If the exclusion is persistent or tips into bullying, ask the school for its anti-bullying policy in writing and work through it with the class teacher, the playground supervisor or the school counselor. Free confidential help is available: in the US, the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline (call 1-800-422-4453), plus the guidance at StopBullying.gov; in the UK, Childline on 0800 1111.
