ADVENTURE 1

Joke or Real?

At lunchtime Ece looked at Deniz’s enormous sandwich.

“If you eat that, you’ll get as big as an elephant!” she said.

Deniz put the sandwich down. He looked at his tummy. Elephants were big. What if something really did happen?

Ece laughed. “I was joking.”

Deniz’s face went hot. He was cross with himself for not getting the joke.

Pusula landed on the fruit box. “Guessing whether something is a joke can be hard,” she said. “You don’t have to guess.”

Deniz turned to Ece.

“Did you say that for real, or was it a joke?”

“A joke,” said Ece. Then she stopped. “Did it scare you?”

“A bit,” said Deniz. “Could you say it’s a joke first?”

Ece nodded. “All right. Elephant joke coming up: this sandwich is enormous!”

This time Deniz was ready. He could laugh if he wanted to — or he could simply say, “I see.”
