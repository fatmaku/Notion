A Note for Grown-Ups

Friendship is not only talking a lot, making eye contact or joining in a big noisy game. Laughing together, doing separate things at the same table, taking turns to share what you know, and hearing each other’s “stop” — those are friendship too.

While you read this book:

  • Do not turn it into a test of “which is the correct social behavior?”
  • Think about what Deniz needs and what the other child needs, together.
  • Never force a child into eye contact, hugs or crowded games.
  • Try the short sentences in role play, at a calm moment.

Compass for grown-ups: The aim is not for the child to hide himself, but for him to be able to build relationships that are clear, safe and mutual.
