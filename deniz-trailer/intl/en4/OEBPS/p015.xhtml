ADVENTURE 3

The Rocket Waiting Its Turn

In class everyone was talking about the weekend.

Deniz remembered the red fire engine he had seen on Saturday. The siren, the ladder, the wheels... he wanted to tell them all of it, right now.

But there were still five people before his turn.

The sentence inside Deniz worked like a rocket. The longer he waited, the hotter its engine got.

While another child was speaking, Deniz burst out: “I saw a red fire engine!”

The child went quiet.

When Deniz looked at his friend’s face he knew something had gone wrong — but the sentence rocket had already launched.

Pusula gave him a small rocket card. Deniz drew a picture of the fire engine and put the card in front of him.

“Your idea can wait here,” said Pusula.

When his turn came, Deniz looked at his card. The rocket was still there.

First he said to the child who had been speaking, “I’m sorry I interrupted you.” Then he told his own story.
