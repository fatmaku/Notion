ADVENTURE 7

Dinosaur Rain and the Talking Ball

Deniz loved dinosaurs. He brought his brand-new Ankylosaurus fact into class.

Ece said, “I went swimming on the weekend.”

Deniz said, “The Ankylosaurus had a club of bone on the end of its tail.”

Arda said, “I’ve got new glasses.”

Deniz said, “Some dinosaurs had rings of bone around their eyes.”

After a while, everyone went quiet.

Deniz had so much to tell them, and he couldn’t work out why nobody was listening.

Pusula rolled a small talking ball. “Your rain of facts is wonderful,” she said. “But in a friendship, the ball goes back and forth.”

Deniz turned to Ece. “What did you like best about swimming?”

Ece told him. Then she asked, “So what was that tail club actually for?”

Deniz’s dinosaur knowledge hadn’t gone anywhere. And now there was an ear turned toward him.
