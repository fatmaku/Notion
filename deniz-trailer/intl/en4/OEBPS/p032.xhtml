WHAT PUSULA SAYS

A Special Interest Is a Bridge — It Doesn’t Have to Cover the Whole Road

Pusula showed the three moves of a two-way conversation:

  • Tell one piece.
  • Ask a question.
  • Say something that connects to the answer you get.

Deniz asked, “How many minutes am I allowed to talk for?”

“It depends on the person and the moment,” Pusula answered. “You can ask, ‘Would you like to hear about this?’”
