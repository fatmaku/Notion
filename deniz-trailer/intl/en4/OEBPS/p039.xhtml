THE ENDING

My Friendship Compass Is Working

In the park the children set up a game again.

Somebody made a joke. Deniz didn’t understand it, so he asked.

One game was full. Deniz chose his back-up plan.

Arda put the blue carriage somewhere else. They built two separate trains.

Deniz wanted to talk about dinosaurs for a long time. First he said, “Would you like to hear about it?”

Sometimes his friends said “yes”. Sometimes they said “not now”. And sometimes Deniz said “no” himself.

Pusula asked, “So do you know all the rules of the social world now?”

Deniz laughed. “No. The rules keep changing anyway.”

“Then what do you know?” asked Pusula.

“If I don’t understand, I ask,” said Deniz. “I say what I want. I listen to the other person. And if I’m not safe, I move away and get help.”

Pusula’s four directions shone like gold.

“Being a friend doesn’t mean losing myself — it means making room for each other.”
