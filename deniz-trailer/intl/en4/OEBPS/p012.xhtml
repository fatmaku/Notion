WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Joining In Can Be Learned — and Being Turned Down Can Happen

Pusula asked, “If you use the three doors, does everyone have to say yes?”

“I wish,” said Deniz.

“Sometimes a game is full,” said Pusula. “Sometimes children want to play on their own. A ‘no’ does not mean you are worth less.”

They made a back-up plan:

  • Ask about a different role.
  • Look for another game.
  • Start your own game.
  • Take a short break.
