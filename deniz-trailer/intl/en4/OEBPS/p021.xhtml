💜 For Parents and Teachers

Flexibility does not mean the child giving up everything he wants. First understand which part matters to him; then try one small change, offered as a choice. Instead of “stop being stubborn”, ask, “Why is this rule important to you?”

Use clear boundaries in shared play: a personal set-up can be protected, while decisions in the shared section are taken in turn. Timed turns and a clear point at which things are handed back feel safer than forced sharing.
