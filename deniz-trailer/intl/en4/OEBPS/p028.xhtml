WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Listen to the Thought, Then Check It

Pusula asked four detective questions:

  • What did I see or hear?
  • What meaning did my head give it?
  • Could there be two other explanations?
  • If it’s safe, how could I ask?

Deniz said, “And what if she really is cross?”

“Then you look for an open conversation and a way to repair it,” said Pusula. “Somebody being cross with you does not mean you are a bad person.”
