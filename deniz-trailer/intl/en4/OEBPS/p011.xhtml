ADVENTURE 2

The Three Doors into a Game

Out in the playground the children were playing space pirates. Deniz wanted to join in.

He ran straight into the middle of the game. He picked up a shiny stone from the ground.

“This is a Mars crystal now!” he said.

One child shouted, “No! That was our treasure!”

Deniz stepped back. It felt as though the door to the game had shut in his face.

Pusula drew three invisible doors on the playground.

First door: Watch. Second door: Ask. Third door: Offer a role.

Deniz watched for a while. He noticed that the children were guarding the treasure.

“Can I join the game?” he asked. “I could be the space mapmaker.”

One of the children said, “We do need a mapmaker,” and handed him a piece of chalk.

Deniz drew a planet path on the ground. This time he hadn’t fallen into the game — he had come in through the door.
