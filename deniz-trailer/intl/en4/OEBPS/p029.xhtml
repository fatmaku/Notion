💜 For Parents and Teachers

Do not close down a child’s fear right away with “don’t be silly, nobody’s cross with you”. Accept the feeling first, then separate the observation from the interpretation. Make “what happened?” concrete: Which words? Which face? When?

In safe relationships, teach direct and kind checking sentences. If the child seeks constant reassurance about every social signal, the aim is not to supply endless answers, but to build — slowly — his skill at gathering evidence and sitting with uncertainty.
