💜 For Parents and Teachers

Instead of judging a social situation once it is over, break it into small pieces: What was said? What did Deniz understand? What might the other person have understood? Which short sentence would help next time? Generate possibilities without imposing one single “correct reading”.

A child’s quietness, side-by-side play or need for a rest is not a lack of friendship. Mutuality, safety and willingness matter far more than outward appearance.
