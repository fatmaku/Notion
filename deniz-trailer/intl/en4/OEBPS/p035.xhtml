ADVENTURE 8

We’re Friends Side by Side Too

It was “friendship day” in class. The teacher asked pairs to make a picture together.

Deniz and Arda sat at the same table. Deniz wanted to draw a spaceship. Arda wanted to line up his little cars quietly.

The teacher said, “Play together, you two.”

They both tensed up. Deniz hunted for something to talk about. Arda drew his arms in around his cars to protect them.

Pusula laid a long sheet of paper across the middle of the table. On one side she drew a space road, on the other a city road. In the middle she left a shared bridge.

Deniz drew his ship. Arda drove his cars along the city road. For a while they didn’t speak.

Then Arda drove one car across the bridge. Deniz added a small garage to his ship.

They both smiled.

Nobody talked non-stop. Nobody forced their eyes round to look at the other. And still, they were together.
