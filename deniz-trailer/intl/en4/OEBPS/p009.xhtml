💜 For Parents and Teachers

Never say “How can you not get it?” to a child who finds hints, teasing, idioms or tone of voice hard to read. Make it normal to state your intention out loud. In class, work on the difference between a joke and a put-down with one question: “Are both sides safe, and do both sides want this?”

When a child takes something literally, do not laugh at him. Give reassurance first, then show the two possible meanings.
