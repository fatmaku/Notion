💜 For Parents and Teachers

Instead of pushing children into constant face-to-face, high-intensity interaction, offer side-by-side, low-pressure activities with shared materials. Drawing together, a Lego table, collecting things outdoors or quiet reading can all be more reachable ways in to a relationship.

Do not measure friendship only by “how many friends has he got?” One safe relationship — where the child can relax, where his boundary is heard and his interests are met halfway — is worth a great deal.
