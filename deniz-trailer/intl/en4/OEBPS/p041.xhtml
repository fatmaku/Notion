Friendship Compass Cards

Make four cards with no writing on them, one for each direction:

  • The asking direction: A question mark — “Can I ask what you meant?”
  • The saying-myself direction: A speech bubble — “I want this / I don’t want this.”
  • The listening direction: Two ears and a talking ball — “It’s your turn now.”
  • The safety direction: An open door and a grown-up — “It didn’t stop. I’m going to get help.”

Act out short situations with a toy. Let the child pick just one card; don’t expect him to solve every situation from beginning to end. Grown-ups can model their own mistakes too: “I interrupted you. Now I’m going to listen.”

💜 A closing question for grown-ups

“In this relationship, are we expecting the child alone to adapt — or are we making room on both sides?”
