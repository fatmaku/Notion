💜 For Parents and Teachers

Do not treat a special interest as an obsession to be silenced. It can be a source of learning, connection and self-confidence. At the same time, teach the signals of two-way conversation openly: asking permission, keeping a section short, pausing, asking a question, and saving the rest for another time.

Setting up a trusted telling-time at home — a “five-minute expert’s platform” — honours the child’s knowledge and can ease the pressure of treating every moment as the only chance to tell someone.
