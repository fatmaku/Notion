ADVENTURE 6

Is She Cross with Me? The Evidence Detective

In the morning Ece said a quick “hello” to Deniz and went straight into the classroom.

An alarm went off in Deniz’s head.

“She’s cross with me. I talked too much yesterday. She doesn’t want to be my friend any more.”

All day long, everything Ece did got tied to that thought. When she sat with somebody else, the alarm grew louder.

Pusula put on a detective’s hat. “Your thought matters,” she said. “But a thought and a piece of evidence are not the same thing. Let’s open the file.”

They made two columns.

She might be cross: She only spoke briefly. There might be another explanation: She was in a hurry, she was tired, the teacher called her.

Deniz asked Ece, “You only said a quick hello this morning. Are you cross with me?”

“No,” said Ece. “I’d forgotten to finish my homework. I was in a real panic.”

The alarm switched off.

Sometimes the alarm might turn out to be right. But a detective collects the evidence first.
