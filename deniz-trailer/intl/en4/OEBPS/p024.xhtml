WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Feelings Are Free — Doing Harm Is Not

“You’re allowed to be sad about losing, to be cross, or not to want another go,” said Pusula. “You just need a plan so that people and things don’t get hurt.”

Deniz’s earthquake plan:

  • Take one step away from the game.
  • Give your hands a safe job: push the wall, squeeze a ball.
  • Say the short sentence.
  • Once you’re calm, repair it or choose again.
