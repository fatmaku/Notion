ADVENTURE 5

The Earthquake Inside Me When I Lose

At playtime Deniz and his friends had a tower race.

Deniz’s tower toppled just as it was about to get really tall. Ece’s tower stayed standing.

“I won!” said Ece.

Inside Deniz, the ground shook. His hands went hot. His ears roared. He didn’t want to build a new tower — he wanted to knock every single block down.

He threw one block hard at the floor.

Pusula did not say “calm down”. First she showed him the safe space.

Deniz took two steps back, pushed his hands against the wall and breathed out strongly three times. The earthquake slowed down.

Then he looked at the game. “Losing was really hard for me. Throwing the block wasn’t right.”

Ece asked, “Do you want another round?”

Deniz checked his body. “Not now. In a little while.”

That was a choice too.
