WHAT PUSULA SAYS

To Wait, Put Your Idea Somewhere Safe

“Waiting your turn is not only about being quiet,” said Pusula. “It’s about making room for someone else without losing your idea.”

Deniz chose three tools:

  • Draw or write one single word.
  • Hold up one finger as a “say it later” sign.
  • At the right moment, say, “When you’ve finished, can I tell mine too?”
