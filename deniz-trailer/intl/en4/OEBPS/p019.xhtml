ADVENTURE 4

My Rule, Our Game

Deniz and Arda were building a toy train.

As far as Deniz was concerned, the blue carriage always belonged right behind the engine. That was the fastest way.

Arda put the blue carriage at the very end.

Deniz put it straight back.

Arda moved it to the end again.

“You’re doing it wrong,” said Deniz.

“It’s my train too,” said Arda, and he walked away from the game.

Deniz was baffled. Why would you do it another way when there was a right way?

Pusula drew two tracks. One had a “my choice” symbol on it, the other a “shared choice” symbol.

Deniz went over to Arda. “I like the blue carriage at the front. Where do you want it?”

“At the back.”

They built two trains. Then they brought the trains together at a station in the middle.

Deniz’s rule hadn’t been lost. And Arda’s game hadn’t been squashed.
