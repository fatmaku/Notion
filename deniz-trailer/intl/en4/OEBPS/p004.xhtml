WHAT PUSULA SAYS

Four Directions in Friendship

Pusula opened her wings to all four sides:

  • Ask: If you didn’t understand, ask for an explanation.
  • Say: Tell people what you want, or what you don’t want.
  • Listen: Notice the other person’s words and their boundaries.
  • Move away and get help: If it isn’t safe, you don’t have to stay in the game.

Deniz said, “Can I do all of these every single time?”

“No,” said Pusula. “Friendship isn’t a performance. It’s practice.”
