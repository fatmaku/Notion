## Research notes

### Market size
- Live streaming market 2026: $97B–$157B, growing toward $250–345B by 2030 (market.us CAGR 26.7%; gyre.pro). [1, 2]
- Creator economy 2026: ~$216B (datarefs) / $260B (newmarketpitch, CAGR 22%); live streaming ≈ 14% ≈ $36B of it. [3, 4]
- TikTok LIVE fastest-growing: >100M creators went LIVE across SEA/Caucasus/Central Asia in 2025 (+77% YoY). [2]

### TikTok LIVE gifting
- Gifts ≈ 50% of streamers' income; average $5,000 per stream cited (influencerfee / muvi – treat as upper range); 20k–100k follower creators: $500–$3,000/month from gifts; creators keep ~50% of gift value; >60,000 US TikTok LIVE creators earn more than median part-time income. [5, 6, 7]
- Turkey: 1 TikTok coin ≈ 0.37–0.43 TL (70 coins 29.90 TL; 7,000 coins at 0.37 TL/coin); TikTok takes ~50% commission; LIVE requires 1,000 followers + age 16 (18 for gifts). [8, 9, 10]

### Competitors / pricing
- Streamlabs: free core; Ultra $27/month (multistream, alerts, overlays), Ultra+ $79/month. StreamYard Core $35/month; Restream Standard $16/month. [11, 12]
- Voicemod: free + Pro $10/month (voice changer + soundboard, 200+ voices, 100+ effects). [13]
- Gap: all of these are viewer-triggered (alerts on donations/follows) or manual soundboards; none reacts to what the streamer SAYS.

### Platform routes
- TikTok stream key: needs 1,000 followers + 18+; key changes every session; TikTok LIVE Studio = official free Windows app (no Mac), no stream key needed; OBS/Streamlabs/XSplit via custom RTMP. [14, 15, 16]
- Instagram Live Producer: go live from desktop with OBS/Streamlabs via stream key, custom graphics allowed. [17, 18]

### Vision 2027–2029 (sources 19–60, notes)
- Corrections applied in the spec: audiobook market DE 374 M€ (Börsenverein) instead of a Statista forecast; Duolingo figures as of Q2 2026 (SEC filing); Turkish-origin population per Destatis 2025 = 2.65 M people with migration history from Turkey; cost anchor = Google Veo list prices (not Sora 2). [19, 32, 38, 24]
- Video AI cost: $0.05/s (Veo 3.1 Lite, 720p) to $0.75/s → $180–2,700 per hour of companion video; LiveFX $0 without AI, $0.25–0.75/h with optional text AI (project measurement) → 240× to >10,000× cheaper. [24, 25]
- Language learning: in-app $1.54B 2025 (+18.8%); total incl. classroom ≈ $84B; Duolingo 58.7M DAU / 12.7M paid (Q2 2026); Lingokids raised $120M (2025). [32–35]
- Germany education: 307k new integration-course participants 2025, 17,204 courses, 18,920 teachers, 30.1% Ukrainian; 20.4% of pupils <16 speak another family language; 25% of 4th graders below reading minimum; DigitalPakt 2.0 €5B over five years from 1 Sep 2026; ANTON school licence €250–700/yr. [36–41]
- Editing: video editing software $2.52B → $2.68B (2025 → 2026); AI video generation & editing $3.67B 2026 → $24.89B 2036; CapCut 736M mobile MAU, >$1B IAP 2025; OpusClip >10M users, $215M valuation, ARR est. $10–20M; price anchors OpusClip $15–29, Submagic $19–69, Descript $16–65; Canva acquired Cavalry + MangoAI (Feb 2026). [23, 51–56]
- XR/audio: smart glasses 13.6M units / $5.1B 2026 (IDC); Meta Ray-Ban Display open to web apps (May 2026); tonies €630M 2025 (+31%); Resolume Avenue €299 / Arena €799. [45, 46, 49, 50]
- Web platform: WebGPU in all major browsers; Chrome 139 on-device speech recognition (`processLocally`); WebCodecs Chrome/Edge 94+, Firefox 130+, Safari 26 with AudioEncoder; Mediabunny muxer; whisper-web (WebGPU). [27, 28, 57–60]
