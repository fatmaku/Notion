## 🇩🇪 Deutsch

Dieser Ordner enthält alles, was LiveFX nach außen vorstellt: Vision, Businessplan, Marktanalyse, Pitch-Deck, Trailer, Prototypen, LinkedIn-Texte und Landingpage. Die Produktdokumentation liegt eine Ebene höher (`../README.md`, `../CHANGELOG.md`, `../docs/`).

**Die Vision in einem Satz:** „Du redest. Es wird Bild.“ Vier Linien, alle im Browser gezeichnet statt in der Cloud generiert: **A Erzählfilm** (Live-Video aus Erzählen und Vorlesen), **B WortBild** (Sprachenlernen: Wort → Bild + Zielsprache + Aussprache), **C LiveFX Räume** (Bühne, Klassenzimmer, Hörbuch, Podcast, AR/VR, Brille), **D LiveFX Studio** (Live-Schnitt und Auto-Edit fertiger Videos). Details: `VISION.md`.

### Typische Abläufe

- **Investor oder Förderstelle:** `BUSINESSPLAN.docx` + Pitch-Deck (als PDF exportiert) + Link zu Haupt- und Vision-Trailer. Vorher Platzhalter prüfen: `grep -n "\[" BUSINESSPLAN.md`.
- **Plattform-Pitch (TikTok, Meta, YouTube):** Pitch-Deck, Vision-Trailer 16:9, `../PITCH.md` als Einseiter.
- **Bildung, Verlage, Integration:** `VISION.md` Kapitel 5 (WortBild) + Prototyp `prototypes/sprachlernen.html` live zeigen.
- **LinkedIn:** Reihenfolge und Hashtags in `LINKEDIN.md`; Vision-Post mit dem Vision-Trailer 9:16.
- **Zahlen aktualisieren:** neue Quelle in `QUELLEN.md` (nächste Nummer) und in allen drei Sprachfassungen nachtragen; zitieren als [Quelle n] / [Kaynak n] / [Source n].

### Werkzeuge und Regenerierung

Die Word- und PowerPoint-Dateien werden aus Quelltexten erzeugt (`tools/`, Anleitung in `tools/README.md`). Einmalig: `npm i docx pptxgenjs --prefix tools`.

```bash
cd live-fx/business
# Businessplan (Word) – Master und Übersetzungen
node tools/build-docx.js BUSINESSPLAN.md    BUSINESSPLAN.docx    de
node tools/build-docx.js BUSINESSPLAN.tr.md BUSINESSPLAN.tr.docx tr
node tools/build-docx.js BUSINESSPLAN.en.md BUSINESSPLAN.en.docx en
# Pitch-Deck – Folientext in tools/deck-content.<de|tr|en>.json
node tools/build-pptx.js tools/deck-content.de.json LiveFX_Pitch.pptx
node tools/build-pptx.js tools/deck-content.tr.json LiveFX_Pitch_TR.pptx
node tools/build-pptx.js tools/deck-content.en.json LiveFX_Pitch_EN.pptx
```

Trailer: Render-Befehle stehen in `VIDEO.md` (Haupt-Trailer, `video/capture.js`) und `video/VISION-TRAILER.md` (Vision-Trailer, `video/capture-vision.js --lang=de|tr|en`). Vorschau im Browser: `video/vision.html?ratio=16x9&lang=tr`. Prototypen brauchen nichts: Datei im Browser öffnen.

Platzhalter für noch fehlende Angaben: `[Zahl]`, `[E-Mail]`, `[Telefon]` – nichts erfinden, vor dem Versand ausfüllen.

Bu klasör LiveFX’i dışarıya tanıtan her şeyi içerir: vizyon, iş planı, pazar analizi, sunum, fragmanlar, prototipler, LinkedIn metinleri ve tanıtım sayfası. Ürün belgeleri bir üst klasörde (`../README.md`, `../CHANGELOG.md`, `../docs/`).

Ayrıntılar: `VISION.tr.md`.

Türkçe dosyalar yukarıdaki tablonun **TR** sütununda: `VISION.tr.md`, `BUSINESSPLAN.tr.md` / `.tr.docx`, `MARKTANALYSE.tr.md`, `QUELLEN.tr.md`, `PITCH-DECK.tr.md`, `LiveFX_Pitch_TR.pptx`, `video/LiveFX_Trailer_tr_*.mp4`, `video/LiveFX_Vision_tr_*.mp4`. `LINKEDIN.md`, `VIDEO.md`, `video/VISION-TRAILER.md` ve `prototypes/README.md` dosyalarında ayrı bir 🇹🇷 Türkçe bölümü var. ### Sık kullanılan akışlar

- **Yatırımcı veya destek kurumu:** `BUSINESSPLAN.tr.docx` + `LiveFX_Pitch_TR.pptx` (PDF olarak) + iki fragmanın bağlantısı. Göndermeden önce yer tutucuları doldur: `[Sayı]`, `[E-posta]`. Kurucu & Mucit, Genel Müdür: Tuncay Sancak · Investor Relations: Gönül Demet · ön tohum ihtiyacı: en fazla %10 karşılığında 250.000 € (yatırım öncesi 2,25 milyon €, 18 ay; `BUSINESSPLAN.tr.md` 11.6).

## 🇬🇧 English

This folder holds everything that presents LiveFX to the outside world: vision, business plan, market analysis, pitch deck, trailers, prototypes, LinkedIn copy and landing page. Product documentation lives one level up (`../README.md`, `../CHANGELOG.md`, `../docs/`).

**The vision in one sentence:** “You talk. It becomes a scene.” Four lines, all drawn in the browser instead of generated in the cloud: **A Story Film** (live video from storytelling and reading aloud), **B WordPicture** (language learning: word → picture + target language + pronunciation), **C LiveFX Spaces** (stage, classroom, audiobook, podcast, AR/VR, smart glasses), **D LiveFX Studio** (live editing and auto-edit of finished videos). Details: `VISION.en.md`.

The English files are in the **EN** column of the table above: `VISION.en.md`, `BUSINESSPLAN.en.md` / `.en.docx`, `MARKTANALYSE.en.md`, `QUELLEN.en.md`, `PITCH-DECK.en.md`, `LiveFX_Pitch_EN.pptx`, `video/LiveFX_Trailer_en_*.mp4`, `video/LiveFX_Vision_en_*.mp4`. `LINKEDIN.md`, `VIDEO.md`, `video/VISION-TRAILER.md` and `prototypes/README.md` each have a separate 🇬🇧 English section. The prototypes and the landing page have a language switch at the top right (`landing.html#lang=en`).

### Typical workflows

- **Investor or funding body:** `BUSINESSPLAN.en.docx` + `LiveFX_Pitch_EN.pptx` (exported as PDF) + links to both trailers. Fill in the placeholders `[Number]`, `[Email]` first. Founder & Inventor, Managing Director: Tuncay Sancak · Investor Relations: Gönül Demet · pre-seed ask: €250,000 for at most 10% (€2.25M pre-money, 18 months; `BUSINESSPLAN.en.md` 11.6).
- **Platform pitch (TikTok, Meta, YouTube):** pitch deck plus the 16:9 vision trailer.
- **Education, publishers, integration programmes:** `VISION.en.md` chapter 5 (WordPicture) plus a live demo of `prototypes/sprachlernen.html`.
- **Updating figures:** add the new source to `QUELLEN.md` (next number) and to all three language versions; cite as [Source n].

### Tools and regeneration

Word and PowerPoint files are generated from source text (`tools/`, instructions in `tools/README.md`, including an English section). One-off setup: `npm i docx pptxgenjs --prefix tools`.

```bash

```

Render commands for the trailers are in the English sections of `VIDEO.md` and `video/VISION-TRAILER.md` (`--lang=en`).
