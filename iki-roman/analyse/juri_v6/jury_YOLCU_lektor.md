# Jury: YOLCU – 1453 Uyanışın Bedeli (Verlagslektor perspective)

## 1. Scores

| | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| **L** | **8.5** | Unusually fair: almost every "sign" is planted and paid off (3D scan ch.5→30, call forwarding ch.7→45, Leyla's note ch.23→box swap + motorised deadbolt ch.45), and ages/timeline hold (42/35/31/20/19; Duru 17/14/10/6; Cem 35/8). A few small slips; one thin joint (the narrator's amnesia). |
| **M** | **8.5** | Disciplined and accurate (Mâide 5:31, Attâr, ibnü'l-vakt, Bakara 2:31, Deccal, nigredo/albedo/rubedo). Signs are rationalised, with one deliberate residue kept. Ch.50's fenâ/return passage is earned, though earlier the spiritual layer is often lectured, not dramatised. |
| **S** | **7.5** | Distinctive, witty, nearly error-free voice and strong set pieces (ch.16, 25, 29, 38, 48), but legal ballast, essay inserts and two interrogation-style solution chapters brake the pace, and everyone speaks in one aphoristic register. |
| **G** | **8.0** | Professional and outstanding in design (three working acrostics, companion architecture). A firm structural edit cutting about 10% would lift it half a point. |

**Stands alone? Yes.** The crime plot resolves completely. ŞAHİT material is framed as Sefa Abi's manuscript; only ch.50's "Kırılmış/Kapak" stays opaque.

## 2. Strengths

- **Inner parliament as voice and plot device:** ch.2 "MUHASEBECİ: Yoğurt ısınıyor." Funny, psychologically functional, and paid off at the deathbed (ch.48 "Hepsi seni yaşatmaya çalıştı.").
- **Pattern-seeking that corrects itself:** ch.11 LİS/VİS, "Bir örüntü daha buldun … Yanlış olanını."
- **Theme enacted, not just stated:** ch.17 "KULLANICIYA UYGUN DÜŞMAN SEÇ."; the book even audits its own preface (ch.47, Duru: "Bu onların cümlesi").
- **Family scenes with moral weight:** ch.13 "Ama kaybolmayacağına söz verebilirsin."; ch.35 "Gelirken bir de ekmek al."; ch.46 "Hatıra bırak."
- **Acrostics seeded inside the story** (ch.1, the riddle notebook): missing chapters give SENİ BUL, the equal-ended knots give BENİ ARAMA, the return arrows give BAŞA DÖN. The "Yarım sesler" device adds a fourth layer.

## 3. What still separates it from a 10 (ranked)

1. **[Structural] The solution comes as interrogation.** Ch.40 and ch.45 resolve about 20 clues as question-and-answer lists ("“Duman?” “Çatı arasındaki fareleri avlıyordu.”"; "“Mavi uçlu tüy?”"). Fair but inert: move a third of the answers into earlier discovery scenes and keep only the charged ones (Leyla's death, Cem).
2. **[Structural / line edit] Legal-procedural ballast.** Chain-of-custody and authority caveats recur in ch.4, 11, 15, 21–23, 25, 31, 37 and 42 ("Bu bir kolluk araması değildi: Mülk sahibi, icra yoluyla…", ch.15; "Kararın kapsamı için o sabah hukuken çizilmiş dar bir sınırdı", ch.42). State the point once, then cut 40–50%.
3. **[Structural / taste] Essay inserts inside confrontations.** Ch.20 interrupts the betrayal scene with a seminar on fortune-telling, placebo and crystals ("Plasebo ve nosebo dediğimiz alan…"). The same applies to ch.26 (the Ekin/Sora case studies) and ch.38 ("Emanet Edilen İrade"). Shorten them or move them.
4. **[Prose / taste, line edit] Too many aphorisms, one voice for everyone.** Every character speaks in maxims, including 12-year-old Emir ("Çalışmak bıraktığın anlamına gelmiyor."). Verbal tics: "ilk kez" 37 times, "…anlamına gelmiyordu" 13 times, "Bazen" about 49 times. Cut maxims by a third; differentiate Zeynep, Nehir and Emir.
5. **[Structural, moderate] The weakest logical joint.** The narrator forgets Yusuf, the white book he compiled and the buyer of his father's shop for twenty years (ch.37 "Yusuf Karaca"; ch.40 "Hipnoz, ilaç veya hafıza silme makinesi yoktu."). This needs one concrete mechanism shown on the page. Ch.35 also gives away the ch.40 reveal ("ikincisinde Yusuf'la beyaz kitabın sayfalarını tasnif ediyor").
6. **[Taste] A repeated device.** The children keep solving technical problems remotely (ch.22, 30, 31, 42, 43: "Kutuyu sağa yatırın, sonra iki kez hafif vurun."). Duru's keyword-triggered voice-bot (ch.35) strains belief.
7. **[Small] A cheap fake-out:** ch.22 "Silah sesi gibi.", resolved within a page.
8. **[Small, taste]** Ch.49 spells out the acrostic rules ("Eşit uçlu düğümün anlatıldığı her paragraftan…"). The ch.1 setup is enough.
9. **[Small, theology]** In ch.50, "Sonra yeniden bir kapı sezdim … Öte yanında yaşayacağım hiçbir şeyi bilmiyordum" can be read as tenasüh (reincarnation), which works against the book's careful Islamic frame (ch.38). One clarifying sentence would fix it.

## 4. Outright errors

- **Ch.39 contradicts ch.5:** "Leyla'nın Berlin'de sorduğu soru … Hangi halkın hangi şamanı?" In ch.5 she asks it "son yüz yüze görüşmemizde", not in Berlin.
- **Envelope inscriptions:** ch.1 "zarfın üstüne yalnızca GEREKİRSE yazmıştım", ch.4 "Üstlerine isimlerini yazdım." and ch.28 "‘Ben açamazsam ölüm belgesiyle açın.’" cannot all be true. Delete "yalnızca".
- **Ch.12 timing:** the train leaves "İstanbul'dan Edirne'ye, 14.53" and he returns "aynı akşam", yet he catches the "Son beş dakikasına" of Duru's school show. The round trip takes about 8 hours.
- **Ch.4 vs ch.44:** "yaşım için fena olmadığımı" and an appointment "Geçen hafta" do not fit "Cihazı iki ay önce … takmışlardı".
- **Red notebook:** ch.1 "Duru için kırmızı bir defter" collides with the missing red notebook in ch.30–31. Change its colour.
- **Ch.42:** "Tünelin çıkışında Nehir bekliyordu." Nehir was in the group leaving by the west door.
- **Ch.40:** "Tarık'ın bulduğu yeni yapıştırıcı tabakası" is never shown in ch.36.
- **Ch.21:** in "Çocuğun kayıp. Kavganızı sonra yaparsınız." the speaker is unclear, and "Kavganızı" doesn't fit Nehir.
- **Typo, ch.40:** "desteği itaattan" should be "itaatten" (compare "itaatle" in ch.24).
- **Nits:** ch.11, "SERVİSİ" ends in -İ, so "VİS" is only its remnant if the final İ fell off too. Ch.2, "Sol elimde iki poşet" is followed by "Ellerimdeki poşetleri".

Otherwise the text is orthographically very clean.
